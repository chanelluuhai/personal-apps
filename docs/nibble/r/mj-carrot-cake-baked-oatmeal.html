Carrot Cake Baked Oatmeal

From MJ and Hungryman · 2026-07-20 · 45 min

Ingredients

  • 2 medium overripe bananas
  • 2 cups rolled oats (200g)
  • 2 tablespoons peanut butter (or nut butter of choice)
  • 1 tablespoon chia seeds (I love this brain booster mix) (use code "MIN20" for 20% off)
  • 2 teaspoons ground cinnamon
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1 cup grated carrots (about 2 medium carrots)
  • 1/4 cup chopped walnuts
  • 1/4 cup raisins
  • 1 1/2 cups milk of choice
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • Greek yogurt for topping

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C). Lightly grease or line an 8 x 8-inch baking dish.
  2. Grate the carrots. Finely grated carrots blend right into the oatmeal, while coarsely grated carrots add more texture. We usually go with the larger grate. Set it aside.
  3. Add the bananas to the baking dish and mash them until smooth. Add the rest of the ingredients and stir everything together until well combined and easily distributed.
  4. Bake for 30–35 minutes, or until the center is set and the top is lightly golden.
  5. Let cool for 10–15 minutes before slicing. Serve warm or chilled with a dollop of Greek yogurt.
