Healthy Banana Oat Cake for Toddlers

From @toddler.snack.therapy on TikTok · 2025-09-24 · 25 min

Ingredients

  • 1 ripe banana
  • 120g rolled oats 1 cup milk (250ml) 1 egg
  • 1 tsp baking powder 1/2 tsp cinnamon For the topping: 1 Banana
  • 60g mixed berries

Steps

  1. 1⃣ In a blender: 1 banana, oats, milk, egg, baking powder, cinnamon
  2. Blend until smooth. 2⃣ Pour into a small cake pan "25×15cm" 3⃣ Top with banana slices + berries. 4⃣ Bake at 180°C for 20–25 min.
