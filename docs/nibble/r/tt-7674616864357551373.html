Cottage Cheese Protein Pizza

From @soniamazri on TikTok · 2026-08-18 · 20 min

Ingredients

  • 1 cup cottage cheese
  • 2 eggs
  • 60g flour
  • ½ tsp oregano
  • ½ tsp Italian seasoning
  • Tomato sauce
  • Shredded mozzarella cheese

Steps

  1. Blend the cottage cheese if desired
  2. Mix with eggs, flour, and seasonings
  3. Shape into individual pizzas or one large pizza
  4. Bake at 350°F (180°C) for 20 minutes
  5. Add tomato sauce and mozzarella
  6. Bake again until the cheese is melted
