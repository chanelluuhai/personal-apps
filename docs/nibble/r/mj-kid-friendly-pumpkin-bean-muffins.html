Healthy Pumpkin Banana Muffins

From MJ and Hungryman · 2024-09-12 · 30 min

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cup all purpose flour, white whole wheat flour, or spelt flour
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder (aluminum-free)
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1 teaspoon cinnamon
  • 1/2 teaspoon nutmeg
  • 1/4 teaspoon ginger
  • 2 medium over ripe banana (240g weighed without the peel)
  • 3/4 cup (180g) unsweetened pumpkin puree
  • 1 egg ( see note)
  • 1/2 cup (90g) canned white beans, rinsed and drained (look for no or low-salt-added)
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 3-4 tablespoons maple syrup or date syrup
  • 1/4 cup dried fruits like raisins, chopped dates
  • 1/4 cup chopped pecans, walnuts

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees Fahrenheit.
  2. In a large bowl, add all the dry ingredients. Stir to evenly combine.
  3. In a food processor/blender, add all the wet ingredients. Puree until smooth.
  4. Combine the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients and combine everything together. Don't overmix.
  5. Divide batter evenly into a greased, lined or silicone 12-cup muffin pan. Bake for 20-25 minutes, until inserted toothpick comes out clean. Allow the muffins to cool for 3-5 minutes before transferring to a wire rack to cool completely.
