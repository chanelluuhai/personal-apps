Baby-led weaning recipes, and easy toddler meal idea

From @mini.tiger.zen on TikTok · 2026-10-01

Ingredients

  • zucchini
  • olive oil
  • cheese

Steps

  1. First cut a zucchini into slices then add zucchini slices on a puff pastry, drizzle olive oil, add cheese and herbs of your choice, bake until it gets golden brown.
  2. For adults you can add more cheese and seasoning and enjoy this as a family.
