Toddler Sweet Potato Cheese Sticks

From @busymamaeasybites on TikTok · 2026-07-30 · 15 min

Ingredients

  • 1 medium sweet potato, steamed
  • 2 tbsp flour
  • 2 cheese sticks
  • Olive oil, butter, or egg wash

Steps

  1. Peel & steam sweet potato until fork tender.
  2. Mash and mix in flour until a dough forms.
  3. Divide into 2 balls and flatten.
  4. Place a cheese stick in the center and seal the edges.
  5. Brush with olive oil, butter, or egg wash.
  6. Air fry at 350°F for 10–15 minutes, flipping halfway.
  7. If cheese leaks out, just push it back in after cooking.
  8. Let cool before serving.
