Sweet Potato Waffles

From MJ and Hungryman · 2025-08-25 · 20 min

Ingredients

  • 1 cup mashed sweet potatoes (240g)
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tablespoon ground flaxseeds
  • 1/2 teaspoon cinnamon
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 2 tablespoons smooth peanut butter

Steps

  1. In a bowl, add mashed sweet potato, eggs, and ground flaxseed. Whisk until smooth and well combined. Small lumps are fine.
  2. Lightly grease your waffle iron if needed. Pour in batter and cook according to your waffle maker’s instructions until set and lightly golden.
  3. Let cool slightly before serving. Cut into strips for baby-led weaning or serve whole for older kids.
