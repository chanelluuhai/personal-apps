Easy Air Fryer Sweet Potato Stuffed Bites

From @busymamaeasybites on TikTok · 2026-07-06 · 20 min

Ingredients

  • 1 cup mashed sweet potato
  • ½ cup all-purpose flour
  • Cooked ground beef, seasoned to your liking
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Olive oil

Steps

  1. Mix the mashed sweet potato and flour until a soft dough forms. Knead with your hands.
  2. Roll into balls, then flatten each one.
  3. Add a spoonful of seasoned ground beef and a little mozzarella cheese to the center.
  4. Pinch the dough closed and roll back into a ball.
  5. Place on parchment paper in your air fryer and brush the tops with olive oil.
  6. Air fry at 325°F for 20 minutes, flipping halfway through (10 minutes per side), until lightly golden. The sweet potato bread will turn slightly brown and crack a little.
