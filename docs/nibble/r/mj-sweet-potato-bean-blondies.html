Sweet Potato Bean Blondies

From MJ and Hungryman · 2026-09-18 · 35 min

Ingredients

  • 1 cup (230g) cooked mashed sweet potato
  • 15 ounce can white beans, rinsed and drained
  • 1/4 cup peanut butter or almond butter
  • 1/4 cup maple syrup
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 2/3 cup (75g) white whole wheat flour (or all purpose flour)
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1 teaspoon cinnamon
  • 1/2 cup chocolate chips (optional but highly recommend)

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (180°C). Line an 8x8-inch baking pan with parchment paper or lightly grease it.
  2. In blender or food processor add sweet potatoes, chickpeas, eggs, maple syrup, peanut butter, vanilla. Blend until smooth.
  3. Stir in the flour and baking powder, then stir just until combined. Be careful not to overmix.
  4. Fold in the chocolate chips, if using. Spread the batter evenly into the prepared pan.
  5. Bake for 25-30 minutes, or until the edges are lightly golden and a toothpick inserted into the center comes out mostly clean.
  6. Allow the blondies to cool completely before slicing and serving.
