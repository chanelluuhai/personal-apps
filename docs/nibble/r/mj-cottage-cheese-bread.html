Cottage cheese bread

From MJ and Hungryman · 2025-04-28 · 50 min

Ingredients

  • 1 1/4 cups full fat cottage cheese (300g)
  • 2 eggs
  • 3 tablespoon olive oil (or avocado oil)
  • 2 cups rolled oats (200g)
  • 2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 1/4 cup chopped walnuts
  • 1/4 cup raisins
  • 2 teaspoons chia seeds
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Lightly grease or line a 8x4 inch loaf pan with parchment paper.
  2. In a food processor, blend cottage cheese, eggs, and olive oil until smooth.
  3. Add oats, baking powder, salt, walnuts, raisins, and chia seeds. Pulse just until combined—don’t overmix!
  4. Pour the batter into the prepared loaf pan and smooth the top. The texture will be thick but don't worry!
  5. Bake for 35–40 minutes, or until golden brown and a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.
  6. Let cool before slicing. Enjoy warm
