It’s hearty, packed with nutrients, and super easy to throw together

From @busymamaeasybites on TikTok · 2026-04-16 · 40 min

Ingredients

  • 1 steamed sweet potato
  • 4 eggs
  • 1/4 cup Greek yogurt
  • Garlic powder (optional)
  • Veggies of choice (I used tomatoes, zucchini & mushrooms)
  • Cheese on top

Steps

  1. Mash sweet potato to dish, mix everything else together, pour into a baking dish, top with cheese, and bake at 350°F for 30–40 minutes (until a toothpick comes out clean).
  2. It’s warm, filling, and perfect for fueling those busy toddler days
