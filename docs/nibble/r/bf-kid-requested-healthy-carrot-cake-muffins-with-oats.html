Kid-Requested Healthy Carrot Cake Muffins with Oats

From Baby Foode · 2025-04-03 · 23 min

Ingredients

  • 1 1/4 cups all-purpose flour, (white whole wheat or a gluten-free flour mix)
  • 1/2 cup quick oats (see notes)
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp baking soda
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 1/4 tsp ground ginger
  • 1/4 tsp ground nutmeg
  • 1/2 cup pure maple syrup (see notes)
  • 1/3 cup melted butter or oil - (such as olive oil, avocado oil, vegetable oil or coconut oil)
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 cups carrots - (peeled and grated)
  • 1/2 cup walnuts, (chopped (optional)
  • 1/2 cup golden raisins (optional)
  • 1/4 cup full fat cream cheese
  • 2-3 tbsp pure maple syrup

Steps

  1. Prep: Preheat the oven to 375° F. Spray or line a muffin tin.
  2. Whisk: In a medium bowl, whisk together the flour, quick oats, baking soda, baking powder, salt, cinnamon, nutmeg and ground ginger.
  3. Stir: In a large mixing bowl, add in the maple syrup, butter/oil, eggs, milk, and vanilla extract and mix until smooth.
  4. Combine: Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients and stir until just combined. Do not overmix.
  5. Add Carrots: Gently fold in the shredded carrots, walnut pieces (if using), and raisins (if using).
  6. Fill Muffin Tin: Scoop the batter into the muffin tin 3/4 of the way full.
  7. Bake: Place the muffin tin into the oven, and bake for 18-20 minutes or until just golden brown.
  8. Make Drizzle: Meanwhile, if using the cream cheese drizzle, place the cream cheese in a microwave-safe bowl and heat in the microwave for 20-30 seconds until just warm. Add in the maple syrup and whisk until creamy and smooth. Set aside.
  9. Eat: Let cool for 5-10 minutes, then drizzle with the cream cheese drizzle (optional). If the cream cheese drizzle is too thick, you can heat it in the microwave for 10-15 seconds. Serve and enjoy!
