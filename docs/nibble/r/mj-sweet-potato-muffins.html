Sweet Potato Muffins

From MJ and Hungryman · 2025-05-12 · 30 min

Ingredients

  • 1 cup mashed sweet potatoes (240g), cooked and cooled
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/2 cup plain Greek yogurt (115g)
  • 1/4 cup olive oil or melted coconut oil
  • 1/4 cup maple syrup
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1 1/2 cups white whole wheat flour (210g)
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking sodea
  • 1 teaspoon cinnamon
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1/3 cup chopped walnuts

Steps

  1. Preheat your oven to 350°F. Line or grease a 12-cup muffin tin.
  2. In a large bowl, whisk together the mashed sweet potato, eggs, Greek yogurt, maple syrup, oil, and vanilla until smooth and well combined.
  3. Add the flour right on top, then sprinkle the baking powder, baking soda, cinnamon, and salt over the flour. Gently stir everything together just until combined—don’t overmix.
  4. Fold in the walnuts. Divide the batter evenly among the muffin cups (about ¾ full).
  5. Bake for 18–20 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean. Let cool for a few minutes, then enjoy warm—or cool completely and store for later!
