Almond Flour Tortillas Recipe

From The Picky Eater · 2026-06-16 · 28 min

Ingredients

  • 1 cup almond flour
  • ¼ cup coconut flour
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 1 tsp xanthan gum
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1 egg (room temperature)
  • 2 tbsp water
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • ½ tbsp apple cider vinegar

Steps

  1. Whisk together the almond flour, coconut flour, salt, xanthan gum, and baking powder until well combined.
  2. In a separate bowl, whisk together the egg, water, olive oil, and apple cider vinegar.
  3. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry and mix to form a dough.
  4. Remove and shape into a ball. Allow the dough to rest for 10-15 minutes.
  5. Divide the dough into 4 even balls.
  6. Place one ball in between 2 sheets of baking paper. Roll dough to form a thin circle.
  7. Remove the top layer of baking paper, place a small plate over the dough, and cut to form a neat circle.
  8. Remove plate and repeat steps 6 and 7 with remaining dough.
  9. Heat a large frying pan over medium heat, place a tortilla (dough side down) into the frying pan and remove the bottom sheet of paper.
  10. Cook for 10-15 seconds minutes, flip and cook for another 10-15 seconds minute. Repeat if needed (if the tortilla doesn't feel fully cooked through). Remove and repeat with remaining tortillas.
  11. Store tortillas covered in an airtight container for up to 2 days.
