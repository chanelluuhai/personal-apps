Sweet potato toddler crackers

From @mountain_mothering on Instagram · 2026-08-24 · 20 min

Ingredients

  • 2 cups of flour
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1 1/4 cup of sweet potato puree
  • 4 tablespoons of melted butter
  • 1/3-1/2 cup grated cheese

Steps

  1. Bake at 350 degrees Fahrenheit for 15-20 minutes or until golden brown.
