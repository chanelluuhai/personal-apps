Disney Dole Whip Recipe

From The Picky Eater · 2026-09-28 · 15 min

Ingredients

  • 2 cups frozen pineapple (see note for creamier option)
  • 4 tablespoons full fat coconut milk
  • 1-2 tablespoons sweetener (optional, dry sugar works better for texture)
  • Use 1 cup frozen pineapple and 1 cup frozen mango (see note for creamier option)
  • Chopped pineapple
  • Chopped cucumber
  • Chopped jicama
  • Chopped mango
  • Chamoy syrup
  • Tajin (or any chile lime seasoning)
  • Dried mango slice

Steps

  1. Put frozen fruit into a blender.
  2. Pulse to chop into small pieces, you almost want it shredded. Make sure you pulse and not blend so that it doesn't get stuck.
  3. Add the milk.
  4. Blend or pulse on low while stopping to move the mixture around. If you have a vitamix, the tamper will work well for this. Do not blend on high or the heat will melt the mixture.
  5. Once it is the consistency of a thick frozen yogurt, pour into a bowl and serve! Top with Chile Mango toppings or any toppings you want!
