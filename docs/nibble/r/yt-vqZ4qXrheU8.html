High protein vegan flaxseed pudding

From @thepickyeater on YouTube · 2025-07-02 · 180 min

Ingredients

  • flax seeds
  • cinnamon
  • milk
  • 1 tablespoon of maple syrup
  • 1 teaspoon of vanilla
  • berries
  • bananas
  • yogurt

Steps

  1. Just mix 3 fourths cup of ground flax seeds, 1 fourth double spoon cinnamon, 1 and 1 fourth cups of unsweetened almond milk, 1 tablespoon of maple syrup, and 1 teaspoon of vanilla extract.
  2. In a bowl, whisk it all up until smooth, refrigerate it for at least 3 hours, or overnight.
  3. When it's ready, you can add your favorite toppings like fresh berries, crushed nuts, bananas, maple syrup, or coconut yogurt that you'll have a fiber packed.
