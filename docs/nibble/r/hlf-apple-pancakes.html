Apple Pancakes

From Healthy Little Foodies · 2018-07-01 · 30 min

Ingredients

  • 40g (¼ cup) Raisins
  • 2 Apples, finely chopped (roughly 0.5cm cubed) (I used Pink Lady)
  • 1 tsp Cinnamon
  • 2 tsp Coconut Oil (* can substitute with butter)
  • 60g (½ cup) Plain (all purpose) Flour (* SEE NOTE 1)
  • 60g (½ cup) Wholemeal (wholewheat) Flour (* SEE NOTE 1)
  • 2 tsp Baking Powder
  • 180ml (¾ cup) Milk
  • 1 Egg

Steps

  1. Place the raisins in a small bowl and soak with boiling water. (SEE NOTE 2)
  2. Heat a nonstick skillet over a medium heat and add one teaspoon of the coconut oil (or butter). Add the chopped apple and cinnamon, reduce the heat, and saute until soft, around 3 mins. Remove from heat.
  3. In a large mixing bowl combine the flours and baking powder. In a separate bowl mix together the egg and milk. Add the wet ingredients to the dry and stir. Don't over mix, a few small and medium lumps are fine.
  4. Drain the raisins and add to the pancake batter along with the sauteed apples. Stir until just combined.
  5. Heat a nonstick skillet on a medium heat (SEE NOTE 3) and brush with coconut oil/butter. Add tablespoons of the mixture into the pan, I did 4 at a time.
  6. When bubbles rise to the surface, flip and cook the other side until golden.
  7. Repeat until the mixture is finished.
