Lunchbox Pasta Salad

From Healthy Little Foodies · 2026-05-07 · 55 min

Ingredients

  • 300g (10.05 oz) dried pasta (, around 3 ½ cups but will depend on pasta shape used. )
  • 1 x 400g (14 oz) can chickpeas (, drained, rinsed and patted dry.)
  • 1 cucumber (, halved lengthways, seeds removed and sliced into half moons. )
  • 200g (7 oz) cherry or grape tomatoes (, halved.)
  • ½ bell pepper (, finely diced)
  • ¼ cup olives (, roughly chopped)
  • 100g (3.5oz) (around ¾ cup) feta cheese (, cut into 1cm cubes )
  • 1 cup basil leaves (, torn)
  • ⅓ cup (125ml) olive oil
  • ¼ cup (60 ml) apple cider vinegar
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tsp honey
  • ½ tsp dried Italian herbs
  • ½ tsp sea salt / cooking salt
  • ⅛ tsp cracked black pepper

Steps

  1. Bring a large pot of salted water to the boil. Cook the pasta for 2-3 minutes longer than the packet instructions. Drain well.
  2. While the pasta cooks, prepare the dressing by whisking or shaking all the dressing ingredients together in a jar until well combined.
  3. Transfer the warm, well drained pasta to a large bowl. Pour over ½ of the dressing and toss well so the pasta absorbs the dressing as it cools.
  4. Once the pasta has cooled, add the chickpeas, cucumber, tomato, bell pepper, olives and feta.
  5. Add the remaining dressing and toss gently to combine. Taste and adjust seasoning if needed. Add basil when ready to serve / pack.
