Vegan Gluten-Free Stuffed Peppers

From The Picky Eater · 2026-09-28 · 50 min

Ingredients

  • 8 red, yellow, orange or green peppers (, medium in size)
  • 3 cups cooked quinoa
  • 3 cloves garlic (, chopped)
  • 1 ½ cups cooked chickpeas (, rinsed and drained (about one 15 ounce can)
  • 2 cups chopped mushrooms
  • 1 cup fresh chopped tomatoes
  • ½ cup fresh chopped parsley
  • 2 teaspoons dried thyme
  • 1 teaspoon sea salt
  • Drizzle of oil for sautéing (, or broth if oil free)
  • Sprinkle of dairy-free cheese (, optional)

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 350 F/ 175 C
  2. Cut the tops off of the peppers and take out the seeds.
  3. Using a food processor, coarsely chop the chickpeas until crumbled like a meaty texture.
  4. Set aside. Then chop the mushrooms in the same way.
  5. Sauté the garlic in broth or oil until fragrant, about 2 minutes.
  6. Add the mushrooms and cook until the liquid has evaporated, about 5 minutes.
  7. Add the tomatoes, parsley, thyme and salt and sauté until the tomatoes soften, about 5 minutes.
  8. Add in the quinoa and chickpeas and mix until heated through. About 1-2 minutes.
  9. Fill the peppers with the mixture and put on the tops. You can also drizzle with a tiny bit of oil if you want.
  10. Bake for about 30-35 minutes until just browning on top.
  11. Serve and enjoy!
