Tahini Sauce

From MJ and Hungryman · 2025-07-21 · 5 min

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup tahini
  • 1/4 cup freshly squeezed lemon juice
  • 1 garlic grated or pressed (can add more to taste)
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 cup warm water

Steps

  1. In a medium bowl or jar, add all the ingredients.
  2. Whisk everything together until the sauce becomes smooth and creamy. If it looks too thick at first, keep whisking – it will come together. If needed, add a bit more water, 1 tablespoon at a time, until you reach your desired consistency.
  3. Taste and adjust. Taste and adjust seasoning if needed – add more lemon juice for tang, salt for flavor, or a touch more water to thin it out.
