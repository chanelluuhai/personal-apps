These salmon rice rolls are packed with protein, veggies, and healthy fats,…

From @busymamaeasybites on TikTok · 2026-06-29

Ingredients

  • Steamed sticky rice
  • 1 sushi nori sheet
  • Salmon fillet
  • Grated carrots
  • Butter
  • Garlic powder
  • Sesame oil

Steps

  1. Sauté the grated carrots in a little butter and set aside.
  2. Cook the salmon on both sides, making sure it’s fully cooked.
  3. Remove any bones and slice into strips.
  4. Spread steamed sticky rice evenly over the nori sheet.
  5. Add a strip of salmon and the cooked carrots.
  6. Roll tightly, brush the outside with a little sesame oil, and slice into bite-sized pieces.
  7. Every child develops chewing skills at their own pace.
  8. You know your little one best, so if they aren’t chewing well yet, this recipe may not be appropriate for them.
