Hocus Pocus Punch

From The Picky Eater · 2026-10-30 · 7 min

Ingredients

  • 4 cups orange juice
  • 2 cups pineapple juice
  • 1 liter lemon lime soda (or ginger ale)
  • 1/2 cup grenadine syrup
  • 1 cup coconut milk
  • 1 cup ice
  • orange slices (for garnish)

Steps

  1. Combine the orange juice and pineapple juice in a large punch bowl.
  2. Slowly pour in the lemon-lime soda.
  3. Gently pour the grenadine syrup into the punch.
  4. Carefully open a can of coconut milk (don't shake it). Scoop out a few spoonfuls of the thick cream from the top and place them on a plate. Put the plate in the freezer for 15 minutes to firm up the coconut cream. Once the coconut cream is slightly firm, fluff it up with a fork. This will create a cloud-like texture. Gently place the fluffed coconut cream on top of the punch to create a foggy appearance.
  5. Add plastic spiders or worms to the side of the punch bowl for a creepy touch. You can also float orange or lime slices on the surface for a colorful and eerie garnish.
  6. Add ice to the punch to keep it cool and refreshing.
