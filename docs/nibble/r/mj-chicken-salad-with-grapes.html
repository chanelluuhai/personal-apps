Chicken Salad with Grapes

From MJ and Hungryman · 2025-02-24 · 10 min

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked chicken, shredded or diced
  • 1/2 cup diced celery
  • 1/4 cup diced red onion
  • 1 cup seedless grapes, halved
  • 1/4 cup chopped walnuts
  • 1/2 cup plain Greek yogurt
  • 1/4 cup mayonnaise (use avocado oil mayonnaise for a healthier option)
  • 1 tablespoon dijon mustard
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1 tablespoon fresh lemon juice

Steps

  1. In a mixing bowl, add the chicken, grapes, celery, red onion, and walnuts.
  2. In a small bowl, whisk together the Greek yogurt, mayonnaise, Dijon mustard, fresh lemon juice, and salt until smooth.
  3. Pour the sauce into the bowl with the chicken and toss gently to coat everything evenly.
  4. Taste the chicken salad and adjust the seasoning with more salt, dijon mustard, or lemon juice if needed. Enjoy!
