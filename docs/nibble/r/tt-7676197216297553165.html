Would you put an entire avocado into chocolate muffins?

From @soniamazri on TikTok · 2026-08-20 · 20 min

Ingredients

  • 1 ripe avocado
  • ½ cup all purpose flour or oat flour
  • 2 eggs
  • ⅓ cup peanut butter
  • ⅓ cup maple syrup
  • ⅓ cup cocoa powder
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • Handful of chocolate chips

Steps

  1. If using oat flour, blend the oats into a fine flour.
  2. Add the avocado and all remaining ingredients except the chocolate chips, then blend until smooth.
  3. Fold in the chocolate chips.
  4. Pour into a greased muffin tin.
  5. Bake at 350°F for 20 minutes.
