Salmon Orzo

From @soniamazri on TikTok · 2026-03-31

Ingredients

  • Butter
  • 1 small onion, diced
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • ½ can tomato sauce
  • 8 oz orzo
  • 16 oz water
  • Handful spinach
  • 2 salmon filets
  • ¼ cup heavy cream
  • Paprika, garlic powder, oregano
  • Salt & pepper (adjust for babies)

Steps

  1. Sauté onion and garlic in butter.
  2. Add tomato sauce, orzo, and water.
  3. Cook on low, stirring occasionally.
  4. Add salmon halfway through, season, and cover.
  5. Cook until orzo is tender and salmon is fully cooked.
  6. Stir in spinach and cream.
  7. For toddlers: flake salmon and keep seasoning low.
