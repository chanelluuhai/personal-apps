Baked Veggie Nuggets

From MJ and Hungryman · 2024-07-31 · 35 min

Ingredients

  • 2 medium carrots, roughly chopped into small pieces (140g)
  • 1 cup broccoli florets (75g)
  • 1/2 cup peas (65g)
  • 1/2 cup corn (70g)
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 small garlic clove (can use 1 tsp minced garlic or garlic powder)
  • 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning
  • 1 cup breadcrumbs (100g), regular or panko style
  • Optional: 1/2 cup breadcrumbs for coating, 1/4-1/3 cup cheese

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 425° Fahrenheit. LIghtly grease a baking sheet or line with parchment paper or baking mat.
  2. Add carrots and broccoli into a food processor/blender and pulse several times until finely chopped.
  3. Add the rest of the ingredients and pulse until combined and chunky (be careful not to overmix)
  4. Take about 1 tablespoon of the mixture and form into a ball. Flatten slightly into nugget shapes.
  5. Roll in extra breadcrumbs if desired.
  6. Bake at 425°F for 20-25 minutes, flip halfway (see note)
  7. Serve with your favorite condiment, like ketchup, ranch, etc.
  8. Heat oil in a cast iron or non-stick skillet over medium-high heat. Working in batches, add nuggets in skillet and cook about 4 minutes each side, or until golden brown and crispy.
