Pumpkin Muffins with Cream Cheese Frosting

From Weelicious · 2024-10-08 · 30 min

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups all purpose flour
  • 2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons pumpkin pie spice
  • 6 tablespoons unsalted butter, (melted)
  • 3/4 cup brown sugar, (packed)
  • 1 cup pumpkin purée
  • 1 large egg
  • 1/4 cup milk
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 4 ounces cream cheese, (softened)
  • 4 tablespoons unsalted butter, (softened)
  • 1 cup confectioner's sugar
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F.
  2. Place the first 5 ingredients in a bowl and whisk to combine.
  3. In a separate bowl whisk the remaining ingredients.
  4. Add the dry ingredients into the wet and whisk until just combined.
  5. Pour into greased muffins cups 3/4 full.
  6. Bake for 20 minutes. Allow to cool before frosting.
  7. Combine all ingredients and beat for 1-2 minutes, until fluffy. Frost cupcakes and serve!
