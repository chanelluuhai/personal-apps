Shrimp Tomato Cream Udon

From @babymealsfreezer on Instagram · 2026-09-13 · 15 min

Ingredients

  • Frozen udon — 2 portions
  • Frozen shrimp — as much as you like
  • Frozen broccoli — as much as you like
  • Tomato paste — 2 tbsp
  • Milk — 200ml
  • Boiled spinach — as much as you like, optional

Steps

  1. Add the frozen udon, shrimp, frozen broccoli, and tomato paste to your rice cooker.
  2. Pour in the milk and mix lightly.
  3. Cook for 15 minutes, then stop the cooking cycle.
  4. Stir well and check that the shrimp is fully cooked.
  5. Add boiled spinach to taste and mix well.
  6. Cut the udon and shrimp into an appropriate size and texture for your baby.
