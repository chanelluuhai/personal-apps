Strawberry Smoothie Bowl

From The Picky Eater · 2026-07-02 · 5 min

Ingredients

  • 1½ cup frozen strawberries
  • ½ cup frozen banana
  • 1 tbsp chia seeds
  • ¼ cup unsweetened almond milk

Steps

  1. Place all ingredients into a high-speed food processor or blender and blend until smooth.
  2. The strawberry smoothie will be thick and easy to scoop out into a bowl. Top with your favorite smoothie bowl ingredients such as: puffed quinoa, berries, toasted coconut, nut butter, chia seeds or sliced banana.
  3. Enjoy immediately!
