Sweet Potato Rice

From MJ and Hungryman · 2024-09-28 · 45 min

Ingredients

  • 3 cups short or medium grain rice
  • 3 cups water (fill to the line marked inside the rice cooker insert)
  • 2 medium sweet potatoes, see below
  • 1 teaspoon minced garlic
  • 3 tablespoon low sodium soy sauce
  • 2 tablespoon sesame oil
  • 2 teaspoons toasted sesame seeds

Steps

  1. Prep the Rice: Start by soaking the rice in water for 30 minutes. This helps to soften the grains and ensures a fluffy texture. After soaking, rinse the rice thoroughly until the water runs clear.
  2. Prep the Sweet Potatoes: While the rice is soaking, give the sweet potatoes a good scrub to remove any dirt. There's no need to peel them unless you prefer.
  3. Cook the Rice and Sweet Potatoes: Place the rinsed rice in your rice cooker and add water until it reaches the 3-cup line marked inside your rice cooker insert. Place the whole sweet potatoes on top of the rice. Close the lid and cook using the normal setting.
  4. Once cooked, use a spatula to carefully break the sweet potatoes into bite-sized pieces and mix them into the rice. The sweet potatoes will be soft, making them easy to incorporate into the rice.
  5. Enjoy the rice as is or mix together the seasoning sauce and pour over your rice and sweet potatoes. You can also mix in some eggs, chicken, beans, or any protein of choice. Give it a good mix and enjoy!
