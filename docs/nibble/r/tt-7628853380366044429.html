How to make

From @busymamaeasybites on TikTok · 2026-04-15 · 2 min

Ingredients

  • 1/2 banana
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tbsp Greek yogurt
  • 2 tbsp flour
  • Pinch of baking powder
  • Fruit topping of your choice

Steps

  1. Mash + mix the wet ingredients, add the dry, and stir well.
  2. Pour into a microwave-safe cup or dish, cover with a plate, and microwave for 2 minutes.
  3. That’s it!
  4. A warm, fluffy, wholesome treat in minutes
