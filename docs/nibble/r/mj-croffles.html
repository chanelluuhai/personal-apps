Easy Croffle Recipe (Croissant Waffle)

From MJ and Hungryman · 2024-07-11 · 10 min

Ingredients

  • 1 package croissant dough
  • 1/4 cup fillings of choice

Steps

  1. Preheat waffle iron. Depending on your waffle iron, you may need to grease the waffle plates with oil.
  2. Roll out your crescent dough or puff pastry on a lightly floured surface to prevent sticking. Fill with whatever fillings you wish.
  3. Gently roll each triangle dough, starting with the wide end of the dough.
  4. Place the croissants one at a time into the waffle iron and cook for 1.5 to 3 minutes, until golden brown.
