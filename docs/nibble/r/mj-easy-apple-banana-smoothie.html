Apple Banana Smoothie

From MJ and Hungryman · 2024-05-17 · 5 min

Ingredients

  • 1 medium banana, frozen
  • 1 medium apple, cored and sliced into chunks
  • 1/4 cup yogurt of choice
  • 1/2-3/4 cup milk
  • 1/2 teaspoon cinnamon
  • 1 tablespoons hemp seeds (optional)
  • 1 tablespoon peanut butter or almond butter (optional)

Steps

  1. Add milk and yogurt followed by the rest of the ingredients to a high-speed blender. Blend until smooth!
