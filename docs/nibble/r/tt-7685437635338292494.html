Creamy Spinach Pasta

From @soniamazri on TikTok · 2026-09-15

Ingredients

  • 8 oz pasta
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 small onion
  • 3 cups spinach
  • ½ cup pasta water
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 3 oz cream cheese
  • ½ cup Parmesan cheese
  • Salt (1 year+ only)
  • Black pepper

Steps

  1. Cook the pasta and reserve the pasta water.
  2. Cook the onion in butter, then add the spinach until wilted.
  3. Stir in the pasta water, heavy cream, and cream cheese until smooth.
  4. Toss with the cooked pasta, add the Parmesan, and cook until creamy.
  5. Season with pepper and salt if serving 1 year+
