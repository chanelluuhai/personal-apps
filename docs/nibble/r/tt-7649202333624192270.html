These oat flour + Greek yogurt pancakes are officially on repeat in our house

From @busymamaeasybites on TikTok · 2026-06-09

Ingredients

  • 1 ripe banana
  • 1 egg
  • 4 tbsp oat flour
  • 1/4 tsp baking powder
  • 2 tbsp Greek yogurt
  • Handful of blueberries

Steps

  1. Mash the banana, then mix in the egg, oat flour, baking powder, and Greek yogurt.
  2. Fold in the blueberries.
  3. Cook on a lightly greased pan over low-medium heat until golden brown on both sides.
  4. Makes about 4 pancakes.
