Homemade unsweetened applesauce

From MJ and Hungryman · 2024-09-16 · 22 min

Ingredients

  • 2 pounds apples, cored and roughly chopped (with peel on or removed)
  • 3 teaspoons freshly squeezed lemon juice
  • 1/3 cup water
  • 1/4 teaspoon cinnamon (optional)

Steps

  1. Combine all the ingredients in a pot. Bring to a simmer, cover, and cook over low heat, stirring occasionally, until the apples are soft, about 15-20 minutes.
  2. Wait until the apples are cooled then transfer to the blender. Give it quick pulses for chunky applesauce. Blend until completely smooth for apple puree.
