Chocolate Sweet Potato Muffins

From The Picky Eater · 2026-09-30 · 35 min

Ingredients

  • 1 cup (250g) roasted sweet potato, mashed
  • 1 ripe banana (about 120g)
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 tbsp maple syrup (optional for a sweeter muffin)
  • ½ cup (50–55g) oat flour
  • ¼ cup (30g) unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • ½ tsp baking soda
  • ¼ tsp salt
  • ⅓ cup (60g) dark chocolate chips or chunks

Steps

  1. Preheat your oven to 350°F and line a muffin pan with 8 liners.
  2. Add the sweet potato, banana, eggs, vanilla, and optional maple syrup to a blender. Blend until completely smooth.
  3. Transfer to a bowl and stir in the oat flour, cocoa powder, baking powder, baking soda, and salt until just combined.
  4. Fold in the dark chocolate chips, reserving a few for the tops.
  5. Divide the batter evenly between 8 muffin cups and sprinkle the reserved chocolate on top.
  6. Bake for 18–22 minutes, until the tops are set and a toothpick inserted into the center comes out with a few moist crumbs.
  7. Let the muffins cool completely.
  8. Melt the dark chocolate with the coconut oil until smooth. Spoon or drizzle a thin layer over each muffin and let the chocolate set.
