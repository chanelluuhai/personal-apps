Chick-Fil-A Kale Salad (Copycat Recipe!)

From The Picky Eater · 2026-06-29 · 10 min

Ingredients

  • 1 bunch curly kale
  • 3½ oz shredded green cabbage
  • ½ cup roasted almonds (roughly chopped)
  • 4 tsp apple cider vinegar
  • 2 tsp lemon juice
  • 3 tbsp maple syrup
  • 2 tsp Dijon mustard
  • ½ cup extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ¼ tsp pepper

Steps

  1. Prepare the dressing by adding all ingredients into a glass jar, screw the lid on tightly and shake vigorously to combine. Alternatively, you can whisk all ingredients in a bowl.
  2. Set dressing aside and prepare the salad by washing and removing the kale leaves from the stems.
  3. Roughly chop the kale and place in a mixing bowl.
  4. Next, add the shredded cabbage.
  5. Drizzle ½ of the salad dressing over top.
  6. Using clean hands, massage to soften the kale.
  7. Cover and refrigerate for 30 minutes - 1 hour.
  8. When ready to serve add the roasted almonds, reserving some to sprinkle on top, and toss to combine. Add more dressing if desired.
  9. Serve immediately.
