Broccoli chicken patties + Greek yogurt dip + kiwi

From @soniamazri on TikTok · 2026-06-02 · 15 min

Ingredients

  • 1 lb chicken breast
  • ½ cup broccoli
  • ½ cup cheddar cheese
  • ½ cup breadcrumbs or blended oats
  • 1 egg
  • ½ tsp turmeric
  • ½ tsp oregano
  • ½ tsp onion powder
  • ½ tsp garlic powder
  • Pepper
  • Salt (1 year+)

Steps

  1. Blend everything
  2. Form patties
  3. Bake at 375°F / 190°C for 15 min, flipping halfway
