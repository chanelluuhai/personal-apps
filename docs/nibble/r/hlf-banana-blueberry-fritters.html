Banana and Blueberry Fritters

From Healthy Little Foodies · 2017-06-10 · 15 min

Ingredients

  • 2 medium small ripe bananas
  • ¼ cup - ½ cup all-purpose flour (plain flour) (*buckwheat flour works as a gluten-free alternative. )
  • ¼ cup blueberries
  • ⅛ tsp cinnamon (,optional)
  • 1 tsp coconut oil (,for frying)

Steps

  1. Mash bananas and then stir in the flour and cinnamon. Half the blueberries and stir through.
  2. Heat a medium/large non-stick frying pan over medium/high and add the coconut oil.
  3. Reduce to a medium heat and add tablespoon spoonfuls of the batter into the frying pan and flatten slightly.
  4. Fry until golden brown on the first side, about 1 to 2 minutes. Flip and fry for a further 1 to 2 minutes. Transfer to a plate and repeat with the remaining batter.
