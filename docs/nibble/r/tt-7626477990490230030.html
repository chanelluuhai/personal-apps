Chicken potato gratin

From @soniamazri on TikTok · 2026-04-08 · 15 min

Ingredients

  • 1 chicken breast
  • 1 egg yolk
  • 3 small/medium potatoes, peeled and sliced into thin rounds
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • Butter
  • 1 handful mozzarella
  • 1 handful cheddar cheese Seasoning
  • ½ tsp paprika
  • ½ tsp garlic powder
  • ½ tsp onion powder
  • ½ tsp oregano
  • ½ tsp dried parsley
  • Salt & pepper (salt only for 1+)

Steps

  1. Blend chicken, egg yolk, and seasoning.
  2. Form medium meatballs and keep 1 tbsp of the chicken mixture aside.
  3. Air fry the potato slices until cooked through.
  4. Cook the meatballs in butter on medium heat until golden, then remove.
  5. In the same pan, cook the remaining chicken mixture, then add heavy cream and mozzarella.
  6. Stir until thick.
  7. Layer potatoes, meatballs, creamy sauce, then cheddar cheese in an oven dish.
  8. Bake at 375°F (190°C) for about 10–15 minutes, until the cheese is melted and golden.
  9. Finish with parsley.
  10. One meal for the whole family
