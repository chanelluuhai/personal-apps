Creamy Tuscan Chicken

From @soniamazri on TikTok · 2026-08-19

Ingredients

  • 1 chicken breast
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • ½ tsp oregano
  • ½ tsp garlic powder
  • ½ tsp onion powder
  • ½ tsp paprika
  • ½ tsp mustard
  • Juice of ½ lemon
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • ½ small onion
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 2 handfuls mushrooms
  • 2 handfuls cherry tomatoes
  • 1 handful baby spinach
  • ½ cup heavy cream
  • Parmesan (optional)

Steps

  1. Slice and season the chicken
  2. Cook until golden
  3. Sauté onion, garlic, mushrooms, tomatoes, and spinach
  4. Stir in heavy cream
  5. Return the chicken to the pan
  6. Finish with Parmesan
