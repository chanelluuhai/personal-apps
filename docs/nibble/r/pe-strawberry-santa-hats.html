Strawberry Santa Hats

From The Picky Eater · 2026-07-20 · 20 min

Ingredients

  • 20 strawberries
  • 2 bananas (cut into 20 pieces)
  • 1/4 cup whipped cream

Steps

  1. Cut the leaves off the top of the strawberries in an even line.
  2. Stack one strawberry on top of a banana slice, then stick a sucker stick through the bottom to hold them in place.
  3. Top each strawberry with a small dollop of whipped cream.
