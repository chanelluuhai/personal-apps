The best shell pasta

From @soniamazri on TikTok · 2026-01-03

Ingredients

  • 1 small onion, finely diced
  • ½ cup shredded carrot
  • Ground beef
  • Salt & pepper
  • Oregano
  • Paprika
  • Garlic powder
  • ½ cup tomato sauce
  • ½ cup pasta water
  • Handful spinach, chopped
  • ¼ cup heavy cream
  • ½ pack shell pasta, cooked

Steps

  1. Sauté onion and carrot until soft.
  2. Add ground beef and seasonings and cook through.
  3. Stir in tomato sauce and pasta water and let simmer on low.
  4. Add spinach and heavy cream, then fold in cooked shell pasta.
