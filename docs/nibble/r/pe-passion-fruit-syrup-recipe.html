Passion Fruit Syrup Recipe

From The Picky Eater · 2026-06-17 · 140 min

Ingredients

  • 1½ cups water
  • 2-4 tablespoons maple syrup (, can use agave nectar or honey too (see note)
  • 1¼ cup passion fruit pulp with seeds (, about 14-16 average sized ripe passion fruit)
  • ½ teaspoon turmeric
  • dash black pepper
  • 1-3 tablespoons Mezcal (, optional for some smokiness (I used 2)

Steps

  1. Add the water and sweetener to a pot. Bring to a boil, about 5 minutes. Boil over medium heat an additional 5 minutes.
  2. Meanwhile, clean out the pulp from the passion fruit.
  3. Remove from heat and add the pulp, turmeric and pepper. Mix around to incorporate.
  4. Allow to steep for 2-3 hours so the flavor gets infused.
  5. Strain over a mesh sieve into a glass jar, squeezing out the juice as you go. I save the pulp and seeds and freeze them into ice cubes to use in smoothies.
  6. Save and use with cocktails, over ice cream or mixed with water as a juice!
