Sheet Pan Orange Chicken

From MJ and Hungryman · 2026-09-10 · 35 min

Ingredients

  • 1/4 cup low-sodium soy sauce
  • 2 tablespoons honey
  • 1 tablespoon tomato paste
  • 1/2 cup orange juice
  • 1 tablespoon orange zest
  • 1 tablespoon rice vinegar
  • 1 tablespoon sesame oil
  • 2 teaspoons minced garlic
  • 1 teaspoon grated ginger
  • 1 tablespoon cornstarch
  • 1 1/2 pounds boneless, skinless chicken thighs (diced)
  • 1 tablespoon cornstarch
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 tablespoon sesame oil
  • 1 head broccoli florets (cut into florets)
  • 1 red bell pepper (chopped)

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 425°F. Toss chicken with cornstarch, garlic powder, and sesame oil. Spread on one side of a sheet pan. Place broccoli and bell peppers on the other side and drizzle lightly with oil.
  2. Bake for 10 minutes. Meanwhile, whisk together all sauce ingredients.
  3. Remove sheet pan from oven and pour sauce over chicken and the veggies. Toss to coat. Return to oven and bake for 8-10 more minutes, until chicken is cooked through and sauce has thickened.
  4. Serve with rice, quinoa, any grain you prefer.
