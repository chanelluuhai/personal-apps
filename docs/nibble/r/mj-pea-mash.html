Pea Mash

From MJ and Hungryman · 2025-08-11 · 5 min

Ingredients

  • 1 cup frozen green peas (thawed)
  • 1 small avocado
  • 1 hard boiled egg
  • 2 teaspoons lemon juice
  • olive oil (optional)

Steps

  1. In a mini food chopper or processor combine the peas, avocado, and hard-boiled egg.
  2. Pulse until you reach your desired texture—smooth for younger babies, or leave it a little chunky for older babies and toddlers.
  3. Add lemon juice and olive oil if using, and pulse once or twice more to combine.
