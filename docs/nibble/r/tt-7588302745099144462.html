The one-pot dinner I make when I’m exhausted

From @soniamazri on TikTok · 2025-12-26

Ingredients

  • Butter
  • 1 small onion, diced
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • ½ can tomato sauce
  • 8 oz orzo
  • 16 oz water
  • Handful baby spinach, chopped
  • 2 salmon filets
  • ¼ cup heavy cream
  • Salt & pepper
  • Paprika
  • Garlic powder .Oregano

Steps

  1. Sauté onion and garlic in butter.
  2. Add tomato sauce, orzo, and water.
  3. Lower the heat and let the orzo cook on low, stirring occasionally so it doesn’t stick.
  4. When the orzo is halfway cooked, add the salmon filets on top, season with salt, pepper, paprika, and garlic powder.
  5. Cover and continue cooking until the orzo is tender and the salmon is fully cooked.
  6. Stir in spinach and heavy cream at the end until wilted and creamy.
