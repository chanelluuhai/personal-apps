Peel the apples, core them, and cut them into thin slices

From @mountain_mothering on TikTok · 2026-10-04 · 45 min

Ingredients

  • 5 apples (I like tart varieties)
  • 3 eggs
  • 120 g sugar (about ½ cup + 1 tbsp)
  • 1 tsp cinnamon & 1 teaspoon vanilla
  • 150 g soft butter (about ⅔ cup / 10 tbsp)
  • 150 g yogurt (about ⅔ cup)
  • 20 ml olive oil (about 4 tsp)
  • 280 g flour (about 2¼ cups)
  • 10 g baking powder (about 2 tsp)
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 30 g sliced almonds (about ⅓ cup)

Steps

  1. Peel the apples, core them, and cut them into thin slices.
  2. For the batter, put the eggs, sugar, cinnamon, soft butter, and yogurt in a bowl and stir briefly until combined.
  3. Add the canola oil, flour, and baking powder, and beat with a hand mixer until the batter is smooth and creamy.
  4. Gently fold in the apple slices so they are evenly distributed through the batter.
  5. Pour the batter into a round baking pan and smooth the top.
  6. For the topping, mix the sugar and cinnamon and sprinkle it over the cake. Finish by scattering the sliced almonds on top.
  7. Bake in a preheated oven at 180 °C (350 °F), conventional top-and-bottom heat, for about 40–45 minutes, until golden brown.
