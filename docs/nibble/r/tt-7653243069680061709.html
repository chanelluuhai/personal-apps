Steam broccoli and potato until soft 2

From @soniamazri on TikTok · 2026-06-20 · 20 min

Ingredients

  • 6oz broccoli
  • 1 potato
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tbsp shredded cheese
  • 1 tsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp breadcrumbs
  • Salt (1 year+)
  • ¼ tsp black pepper
  • ½ tsp onion powder

Steps

  1. Steam broccoli and potato until soft
  2. Mash together in a bowl
  3. Add egg, cheese, olive oil, breadcrumbs and seasoning and combine well
  4. Form into small balls and lay on a baking sheet
  5. Spray with olive oil
  6. Bake at 360°F for 20 minutes, flipping halfway Serve immediately
