Savory Chicken & Veggie Toddler Pancakes

From @busymamaeasybites on TikTok · 2026-07-25 · 4 min

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp cooked chicken, finely chopped
  • 2 tbsp steamed broccoli, finely chopped
  • 1 tbsp cooked mushrooms, finely chopped
  • 1 egg
  • Small handful of shredded cheddar or mozzarella cheese
  • 3 tbsp whole wheat flour
  • 2 tbsp whole milk
  • Dash of garlic powder
  • Olive oil for cooking

Steps

  1. Finely chop the chicken, broccoli, and mushrooms.
  2. Add all ingredients to a bowl and mix until well combined.
  3. Heat a lightly oiled nonstick pan over low-medium heat.
  4. Scoop about 2 tablespoons of batter per pancake and gently flatten.
  5. Cook for 3–4 minutes per side, or until golden brown and cooked through.
