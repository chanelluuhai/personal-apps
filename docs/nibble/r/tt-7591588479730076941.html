Sweet potato waffles

From @soniamazri on TikTok · 2026-01-04

Ingredients

  • ¼ cup flour
  • Pinch of cinnamon
  • 1 cup sweet potato, steamed & mashed
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tbsp avocado oil or melted butter
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • ¼ cup milk

Steps

  1. Mix all ingredients until smooth.
  2. Cook in a waffle maker until golden and cooked through.
