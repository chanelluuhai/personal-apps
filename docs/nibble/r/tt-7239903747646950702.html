Birthday cake for my baby who’s turning 1 soon

From @mini.tiger.zen on TikTok · 2023-06-02 · 30 min

Ingredients

  • 1/3 cup of grated carrots
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/2 banana, mashed
  • 1 tablespoon of greek yogurt
  • 5 tablespoons of coconut flour

Steps

  1. Bake the mixture at 325 F, for 20-30 min.
  2. Let it cool for at least 30 min before serving.
  3. I used cream cheese & greek yogurt mix for frosting, which is optional..
