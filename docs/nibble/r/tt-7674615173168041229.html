Classic Spaghetti & Meatballs

From @soniamazri on TikTok · 2026-08-17

Ingredients

  • Meatballs
  • 13 oz ground beef
  • ¼ cup breadcrumbs
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tbsp parsley
  • ½ tsp oregano
  • ½ tsp onion powder
  • ½ tsp garlic powder
  • ½ tsp paprika
  • Salt (1 year+) & pepper Sauce
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 shallot
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1½–2 cups passata
  • ½ tsp oregano Pasta
  • 8 oz spaghetti

Steps

  1. Mix and shape the meatballs
  2. Brown them
  3. Make the sauce
  4. Simmer the meatballs
  5. Cook the spaghetti
  6. Toss everything together and serve
