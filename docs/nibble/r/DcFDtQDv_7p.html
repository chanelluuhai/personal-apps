Protein packed strawberry ice cream

From @thepickyeater on Instagram · 2026-08-15 · 60 min

Ingredients

  • 2 cups frozen strawberries
  • 1¼ cups 4% cottage cheese or Greek yogurt
  • 1 tbsp maple syrup
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • Pinch of sea salt

Steps

  1. Add the frozen strawberries, cottage cheese / Greek yogurt, maple syrup, vanilla, and salt to a high-speed blender or food processor.
  2. Blend until completely smooth and creamy, stopping to scrape down the sides as needed.
  3. Serve immediately for a thick, creamy soft-serve consistency, or transfer to a freezer-safe container and freeze for 30-60 minutes for a firmer, scoopable texture.
  4. If freezing for several hours or overnight, let the ice cream soften at room temperature for 10-20 minutes before scooping.
  5. Top with fresh strawberries if desired and enjoy!
