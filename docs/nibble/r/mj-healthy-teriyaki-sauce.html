Healthy Teriyaki Sauce

From MJ and Hungryman · 2025-09-07 · 10 min

Ingredients

  • 1/3 cup low sodium soy sauce
  • 1/3 cup orange juice or pineapple juice
  • 3 tablespoons maple syrup (or honey)
  • 1 tablespoon apple cider vinegar
  • 2 teaspoons minced garlic
  • 2 teaspoons grated ginger
  • 2 teaspoons cornstarch (combined with 2 tsp cold water)

Steps

  1. In a small saucepan, whisk together the soy sauce, juice, maple syrup, apple cider vinegar, garlic, and ginger.
  2. Bring the mixture to a gentle simmer over medium heat.
  3. In a small bowl, stir the cornstarch with water until smooth to make a slurry.
  4. Slowly whisk the slurry into the saucepan. Continue cooking, whisking frequently, until the sauce thickens and becomes glossy (about 2–3 minutes).
  5. Taste and adjust sweetness or tanginess as needed (add a little more maple syrup for sweeter, or vinegar for more tang).
  6. Let cool slightly, then enjoy as a glaze, marinade, or dipping sauce—or store in an airtight jar in the fridge for up to a week.
