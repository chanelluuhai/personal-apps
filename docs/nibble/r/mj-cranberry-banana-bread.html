Cranberry Banana Bread

From MJ and Hungryman · 2024-11-18 · 54 min

Ingredients

  • 2 cups white whole wheat flour (245g)
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1 cup mashed banana (240g), about 2 1/2-3 medium bananas
  • 1/3 cup Greek yogurt (78g)
  • 2 eggs, room temperature
  • 1/4 cup unsalted butter, melted
  • 1/4-1/3 cup maple syrup (see note)
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1 cup fresh cranberries, cut large ones in half (110g)
  • 1/2 cup chopped walnuts

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees Fahrenheit. Prepare a 8x4 loaf pan by greasing it with oil or butter or lining with parchment paper. Set aside.
  2. Mash bananas in a medium bowl using the back of a fork or a potato masher. Try to get it as smooth as possible but small lumps are fine. Measure out 1 cup.
  3. To the same bowl, add all the wet ingredients and mix together until well combined.
  4. In a large bowl, combine all the dry ingredients. Add the wet ingredients and mix until just combined. Be careful not to over mix.
  5. Gently stir in cranberries and walnuts. Evenly distribute the batter into the prepared pan. Bake for for 45-60 minutes, or until the toothpick comes out clean when inserted into the center of the bread.
  6. Cool for 5 minutes in the pan then transfer to a wire rack to cool completely.
