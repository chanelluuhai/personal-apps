Arrange the fig and banana slices on a plate or tray with parchment paper

From @busymamaeasybites on TikTok · 2026-06-29

Ingredients

  • 2 fresh figs, sliced (stem removed)
  • About ¾ banana, sliced (or use a whole banana)
  • Whole milk Greek yogurt
  • 1 @onceuponafarm Tractor Wheel, crumbled

Steps

  1. Arrange the fig and banana slices on a plate or tray with parchment paper.
  2. Spoon whole milk Greek yogurt over the fruit.
  3. Sprinkle with crumbled Tractor Wheel pieces.
  4. Freeze for a few hours until set.
  5. Transfer to an airtight container and store in the freezer. To serve, let thaw for a few minutes before offering to your little one.
