Homemade Cheez-Its Recipe (2 Ingredients)

From The Picky Eater · 2026-10-08 · 27 min

Ingredients

  • 6 slices ultra-thin sliced deli cheese (I use cheddar)
  • salt, to taste

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 275°F and line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
  2. Cut each cheese slice into 4 smaller squares. Place them on the baking sheet about ¾ inch apart.
  3. Once the sheet is full, use a straw to cut a hole in the center of each square.
  4. Bake for 20 to 22 minutes.
  5. Remove the pan from the oven and sprinkle the crackers with salt. Gently blot them with paper towels to remove some of the grease.
  6. Let the crackers cool on the pan before removing them.
