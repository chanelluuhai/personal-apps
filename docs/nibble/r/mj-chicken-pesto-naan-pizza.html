Chicken pesto flatbread

From MJ and Hungryman · 2024-08-07 · 15 min

Ingredients

  • 1 flatbread crust
  • 1/4 cup pesto, store-bought or homemade
  • 1 cup diced chicken
  • 1/2 cup cherry tomatoes, quartered
  • 2 ouncecs fresh mozzarella cheese , sliced

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 425° Fahrenheit.
  2. Place flatbread on a sheet pan and spread pesto on top.
  3. Top with mozzarella, tomatoes, and chicken.
  4. Bake for 10-12 minutes or until the crust is crispy and cheese is melted.
