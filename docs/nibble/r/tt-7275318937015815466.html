I made Tofu Pocket Balls for my one-year-old toddler’s lunch

From @mini.tiger.zen on TikTok · 2023-09-05

Ingredients

  • Firm tofu (extra firm tofu works better)
  • Cooked rice
  • Seasoning of your choice (I used regular soy sauce diluted with water) Below are the simple

Steps

  1. Cut tofu into pieces, and slit each piece to create an opening
  2. Pan fry with oil until golden brown & firm
  3. Put rice into each opening / pocket
  4. Add seasoning on top (I used chives & small portion of regular soy sauce (diluted with water) to enhance flavor)
