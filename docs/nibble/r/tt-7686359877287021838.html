Today we’re making a Spooky Spider Web Pancake

From @busymamaeasybites on TikTok · 2026-09-17

Ingredients

  • 1 small banana
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tbsp all-purpose flour
  • 2 tbsp milk

Steps

  1. Mash the banana, add everything else and mix together.
  2. Cook in a little olive oil over low-medium heat until golden on both sides.
  3. Let cool before decorating!
