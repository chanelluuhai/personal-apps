Cheesy broccoli bites

From @soniamazri_ on Instagram · 2026-06-05 · 20 min

Ingredients

  • 250g broccoli
  • 3 eggs
  • ¼ cup breadcrumbs

Steps

  1. Steam broccoli
  2. Mash
  3. Mix with eggs & breadcrumbs
  4. Bake at 375°F / 190°C for 20 min
