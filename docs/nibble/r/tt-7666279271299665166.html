Breakfast doesn’t have to mean standing over the stove

From @busymamaeasybites on TikTok · 2026-07-25 · 20 min

Ingredients

  • 5 eggs
  • Chopped veggies of your choice (I used steamed broccoli, mushrooms, yellow zucchini, and green onions)
  • Dash of garlic powder
  • 1 spoonful of cottage cheese
  • Small handful of shredded cheese

Steps

  1. Line your air fryer basket with parchment paper.
  2. Add all ingredients and mix together.
  3. Air fry at 350°F for 15–20 minutes, or until the eggs are fully cooked.
  4. Let cool slightly, then slice into toddler-friendly pieces. We use 5 eggs so Mom and Dad each get 2 eggs and our toddler gets 1—an easy breakfast for everyone with just one batch! I love this recipe because I don’t have to turn on the oven, and I can simply dump everything in the air fryer and let it cook while I get other things done.
