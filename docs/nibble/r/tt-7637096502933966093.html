Spinach tortilla egg muffins + carrots + raspberries

From @soniamazri on TikTok · 2026-05-08 · 15 min

Ingredients

  • 4 eggs
  • ½ cup cottage cheese
  • ¼ cup cheddar cheese
  • Handful spinach
  • 1 large tortilla

Steps

  1. Blend everything except tortilla.
  2. Cut tortilla into muffin cups.
  3. Pour mixture on top.
  4. Bake at 350°F / 180°C for 15 min
