Crispy Air Fryer Tempeh

From The Picky Eater · 2026-07-28 · 23 min

Ingredients

  • 8 oz Tempeh (cubed)
  • 1 tbsp Olive Oil
  • 1 tbsp Coconut Aminos (or low-sodium soy sauce )
  • 1 tbsp Maple Syrup
  • ¼ tsp Garlic Powder
  • ½ tsp Smoked Paprika
  • ½ tsp Apple Cider Vinegar
  • ½ tsp Salt
  • ½ tsp Pepper

Steps

  1. Add all ingredients, except for the tempeh, into a mixing bowl and mix well.
  2. Gently fold the diced tempeh into the prepared sauce until combined. Marinate the tempeh for 10 minutes.
  3. While the tempeh marinates: preheat the air fryer to 375 degrees F / 190 C.
  4. Lightly spray the air fryer basket with non-stick cooking spray or brush with olive oil. Arrange the marinated diced tempeh in an even layer, ensuring the pieces don’t touch.
  5. Cook the tempeh for 8-10 minutes, or until lightly golden and crispy.
  6. Remove the tempeh from the air fryer and use it in your favorite dishes or enjoy it as a snack.
