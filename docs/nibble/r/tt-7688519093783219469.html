These are the perfect little fall breakfast and SO easy to make

From @busymamaeasybites on TikTok · 2026-09-23

Ingredients

  • 1/3 cup pumpkin puree - from @Lidl
  • 1 egg
  • 1/4 cup whole milk - from @Horizon Organic
  • 1/2 cup all-purpose flour - from @lidlus
  • 1/2 tsp baking powder - from @Trader Joe's
  • Dash of cinnamon
  • Olive oil or unsalted butter for cooking

Steps

  1. Add everything to a bowl and mix until combined.
  2. Lightly grease your pan and metal Halloween cutters, then place the cutters in the pan and pour in the batter.
  3. Cook on low-medium heat until set enough to flip, then cook the other side until golden.
  4. Carefully remove from the molds and let cool before serving!
  5. Such an easy way to make a regular breakfast feel a little more Halloween-y.
