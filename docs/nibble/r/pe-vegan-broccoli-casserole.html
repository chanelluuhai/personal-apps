Vegan Broccoli Casserole

From The Picky Eater · 2026-06-12 · 50 min

Ingredients

  • 1 cup chopped sweet or yellow onion
  • 2 cups sliced mushrooms (, about 8 ounces)
  • drizzle of olive oil (, or broth if you don’t use oil)
  • 1 pound fresh broccoli (, chopped into larger pieces (see note)
  • 3 cups vegan Alfredo sauce (, or cream sauce of choice (see note)
  • ½ teaspoon sea salt
  • ¼ teaspoon ground black pepper
  • ½ cup vegan parmesan
  • ½ cup breadcrumbs (, gluten-free if needed)
  • 2 teaspoons garlic powder
  • 2 teaspoons dried parsley
  • ½ teaspoon sea salt

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees F / 175 C.
  2. Sauté the onions with oil or broth until soft. Abut 5 minutes.
  3. Add the mushrooms, salt and pepper and sauté for 1-2 minutes.
  4. Add the broccoli, saute for about 5 minutes until browning. If you want it softer, then cover and cook to steam and soften more for 2-3 minutes.
  5. Take off the heat and stir in the cream sauce.
  6. Make the topping by mixing all ingredients in a bowl.
  7. Pour into a square baking dish and cover with the topping mix.
  8. Bake for 15-20 minutes, until the sauce is bubbling and the topping is slightly browning.
  9. Serve!
