Oven Beef Mashed Potato

From @soniamazri on TikTok · 2026-08-23 · 20 min

Ingredients

  • Mashed Potatoes
  • 4 medium potatoes
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 3 tbsp butter
  • 2 tbsp cream cheese
  • ⅓ cup heavy cream
  • ½ tsp oregano Beef Filling
  • 12 oz ground beef
  • ½ small onion
  • 1 carrot
  • 1 bell pepper
  • ½ tsp paprika
  • ½ tsp garlic powder
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • Salt (1 year+ only) Topping
  • 2 handfuls mozzarella & cheddar cheese

Steps

  1. Boil the potatoes with the garlic
  2. Cook the beef with the onion, carrot, bell pepper, and seasonings
  3. Mash the potatoes with butter, cream cheese, oregano, and heavy cream
  4. Layer potatoes, beef, potatoes
  5. Top with cheese
  6. Bake at 400°F (200°C) for 15–20 minutes, then broil until golden
