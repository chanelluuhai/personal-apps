Healthy Pumpkin Brownies

From The Picky Eater · 2023-08-16 · 60 min

Ingredients

  • ¾ cup Whole Wheat Pastry Flour
  • ½ tsp baking powder
  • ¾ tsp salt
  • ⅓ cup brown sugar (Increase the sugar if you want more of a traditional super sweet brownie.)
  • ¼ cup butter
  • ¼ cup unsweetened applesauce
  • 2 flax eggs
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • ¼ cup unsweetened soy milk
  • ¼ cup unsweetened cacao powder
  • 6 tbsp dark chocolate chips
  • ¾ cup unsweetened pumpkin puree
  • 2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • ½ tsp ground cloves
  • ½ tsp nutmeg

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Line a 8×8 baking dish with parchment paper or spray with cooking spray. In a large bowl, combine the flour, salt and baking powder. Set aside.
  2. In another bowl, cream together the sugar and butter. Then mix in the flax eggs, applesauce and vanilla until well combined.
  3. Pour wet ingredients into dry ingredients. Stir together with a spatula just until well combined. Then pour half of this batter into the other bowl. To the first bowl, fold in the soy milk, cocoa powder and chocolate chips.
  4. To the second bowl, stir in pumpkin puree and the spices.
  5. Pour the chocolate batter into the baking dish and spread in an even layer across the bottom.
  6. Then pour the pumpkin batter on top and spread in an even layer.
  7. Bake in preheated oven for 25-30 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted comes out clean. Allow to cool to room temperature before cutting and serving.
