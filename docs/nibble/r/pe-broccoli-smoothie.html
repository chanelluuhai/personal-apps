Easy Broccoli Smoothie

From The Picky Eater · 2024-08-06 · 5 min

Ingredients

  • 1 cup broccoli florets
  • ¾ cup mango (frozen pieces)
  • 1 banana (sliced and frozen)
  • ¾ cup unsweetened almond milk
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • ½ cup plain low fat Greek yogurt (or coconut yogurt for dairy-free/vegan)
  • 1 teaspoon coconut sugar (optional)

Steps

  1. Add all the ingredients to a high-speed blender and blend for three minutes or until smooth.
  2. Adjust sweetness to suit personal taste.
  3. Serve immediately.
