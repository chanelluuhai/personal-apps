Vegan Poutine Recipe

From The Picky Eater · 2026-06-15 · 60 min

Ingredients

  • 1 serving French fries (about 2 pounds of potatoes (homemade or store bought)
  • ½ serving vegan gravy (about 1 cup but can use more for extra saucy)
  • ½ serving cheese curds (about 1 cup (homemade or store bought)
  • parsley or chives (chopped (optional for garnish)

Steps

  1. Make French fries if making homemade by following the recipe directions.
  2. Divide the fries onto four plates.
  3. Top with hot vegan gravy.
  4. Add vegan cheese curds.
  5. Garnish with parsley and chives if preferred.
  6. Enjoy!
