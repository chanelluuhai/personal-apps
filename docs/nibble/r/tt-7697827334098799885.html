Always in caption :) Roasted Veggie & Bacon Pasta Salad

From @mountain_mothering on TikTok · 2026-10-18 · 30 min

Ingredients

  • 1 box (16 oz) pasta
  • 3 whole bell peppers, chopped
  • 1 large red onion, chopped
  • 1–2 cups halved Brussels sprouts
  • Olive oil, paprika, garlic powder, salt, and pepper
  • 3–4 slices bacon, chopped Cook the pasta according to package

Steps

  1. ; drain and cool.
  2. Toss the peppers, onion, and Brussels sprouts with olive oil, paprika, garlic powder, salt, and pepper, top with the chopped bacon, and roast at 350°F for about 30 minutes until tender.
  3. Combine the cooled pasta with the roasted veggies and bacon, then toss with the pumpkin vinaigrette.
  4. Add shaved Parmesan (about a cup) Storage Keeps well in the fridge for 3–4 days in an airtight container—great for meal prep.
  5. Pumpkin Vinaigrette Ingredients • ½ cup pumpkin puree • ½ cup olive oil • ¼ cup apple cider vinegar • 3 tablespoons maple syrup • 1 tablespoon grainy mustard • 1 teaspoon thyme • 1–2 teaspoons chili pepper flakes • 1–2 teaspoons salt and pepper Wisk pumpkin puree, oil and vinegar, maple syrup, mustard, thyme, chili flakes, salt, and pepper until smooth.
  6. Taste and adjust seasoning; thin with a splash of water if needed.
