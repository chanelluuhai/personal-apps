Mango Pineapple Banana Smoothie (Creamy & Healthy!)

From The Picky Eater · 2026-07-08 · 5 min

Ingredients

  • 1½ cup Frozen Mango
  • 1½ Fresh Bananas (chopped (can also use frozen)
  • 1½ cup Frozen Pineapple
  • 2 cups Light Unsweetened Coconut Milk (or almond milk)

Steps

  1. Add all ingredients to a high-speed blender and blend until smooth. Pour into a serving cup and enjoy!
