This blueberry banana skillet pancake is made with simple pantry ingredients…

From @busymamaeasybites on TikTok · 2026-08-03

Ingredients

  • 1 ripe banana
  • 1/3 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 egg
  • Dash of cinnamon (optional)
  • Small handful of blueberries, cut in half or lightly smashed
  • Unsalted butter for the pan

Steps

  1. Mash the banana, then mix in the flour, baking powder, egg, and cinnamon.
  2. Fold in the blueberries.
  3. Butter a skillet and cook over low-medium heat until bubbles form on top, then flip and cook until both sides are golden brown.
  4. Let cool before serving.
  5. Simple ingredients, quick to make, and always a hit!
