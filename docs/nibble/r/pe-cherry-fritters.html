Cherry Fritters

From The Picky Eater · 2026-06-24 · 30 min

Ingredients

  • ¾ cup milk
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tbsp melted butter
  • 1¾ cup all-purpose flour
  • ½ cup sugar
  • 1½ tsp baking powder
  • ½ tsp cinnamon
  • ⅛ tsp salt
  • 6 oz cherries
  • 2 cup oil (for frying)
  • 1 cup powdered sugar
  • 1 tbsp cream
  • ½ tsp vanilla extract
  • ½ tbsp water

Steps

  1. Roughly dice the washed and pitted cherries, set aside in a bowl for later.
  2. Place the milk, vanilla, egg, butter, flour, sugar, baking powder, cinnamon and salt into a high speed blender or food processor.
  3. Blend until a smooth batter has formed. Scrape down the sides several times to ensure all of the batter ingredients have combined.
  4. Transfer the batter to a bowl and stir through the diced cherries.
  5. Heat the oil in a large pot over a medium heat. (Test the temperature of the oil by dropping a small amount of batter into the oil, if it starts to bubble immediately then it is ready).
  6. Using a metal spoon, drop tablespoon amounts of batter into the oil. Add as many as you can fit without overcrowding the pot.
  7. Allow the fritters to cook for 1-2 minutes or until lightly golden brown, gently turn and continue to cook until golden.
  8. Remove and drain on kitchen paper towel.
  9. Repeat with remaining batter. Batter makes approx 12-14 fritters.
  10. Allow to cool completely.
  11. Prepare the glaze by mixing the powdered sugar, cream and vanilla together in a small bowl until smooth. Adjust the thickness by adding some water if needed.
  12. Drizzle the glaze over the fritters and allow the glaze to set before serving and enjoying.
