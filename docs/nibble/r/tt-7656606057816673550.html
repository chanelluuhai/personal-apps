5-Minute Breakfast for You & Your Little One

From @busymamaeasybites on TikTok · 2026-06-29 · 5 min

Ingredients

  • 1 cup whole milk
  • ½ cup rolled oats
  • Dash of cinnamon
  • ½ banana

Steps

  1. Cook ~3-5 mins on low/medium heat until creamy, then let your little one choose their favorite fruit toppings
