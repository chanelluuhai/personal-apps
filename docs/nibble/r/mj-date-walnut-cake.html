Date Walnut Cake

From MJ and Hungryman · 2025-05-19 · 60 min

Ingredients

  • 13-15 medjool dates, pitted (200g)
  • 1 cup hot milk
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/4 cup Greek yogurt (58g)
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1 1/4 cups self-rising flour (175g)
  • 3/4 cup chopped walnuts, divided

Steps

  1. Soak the dates: Add pitted dates to a bowl and pour hot milk over them. Let them soak for at least 30 minutes, until softened.
  2. Preheat oven to 350°F.
  3. Blend: Transfer soaked dates and milk to a blender and blend until smooth.
  4. Mix wet ingredients: Pour the date mixture back into a bowl. Add eggs, yogurt, and vanilla. Whisk until combined.
  5. Add dry ingredients: Add flour over the wet mixture. Gently stir until just combined—don’t overmix! Fold in chopped walnuts.
  6. Bake: Pour the batter into a greased and lined 8-inch loaf pan (or use a cake pan if you’d like). Sprinkle extra walnuts on top. Bake for 30–35 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.
