Dairy-Free Thousand Island Dressing (No Mayo!)

From The Picky Eater · 2026-06-09 · 10 min

Ingredients

  • 1½ cups raw cashews (see note)
  • 1 cup unsweetened plain dairy free milk
  • ¼ cup ketchup
  • ¼ cup distilled white vinegar
  • 1 tablespoon fresh lemon juice
  • 1 – ½ teaspoon sea salt
  • 1 teaspoon hot sauce
  • ½ teaspoon paprika
  • ¼ cup sweet pickle relish
  • ¼ cup chopped red onion (, finely chopped)

Steps

  1. Place cashews, milk, ketchup, vinegar, lemon juice, hot sauce, paprika and salt into a blender and blend on high until nice and smooth.
  2. Add in the relish and onion and pulse until incorporated.
  3. Serve!
