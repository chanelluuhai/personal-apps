Savory Vegetable Muffins

From The Picky Eater · 2026-06-05 · 40 min

Ingredients

  • 2½ cups whole wheat flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • ½ tsp baking soda
  • ¾ tsp salt
  • 1 tsp dried rosemary
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • ½ tsp garlic powder
  • ¼ cup parmesan cheese (grated)
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • 1¼ cup oat milk
  • ½ red onion (finely diced)
  • ½ red bell pepper (finely diced)
  • ½ cup baby spinach leaves (chopped)
  • ½ carrot (peeled and grated)
  • 1 small zucchini (grated)
  • ¼ cup frozen corn kernels
  • 2 oz sun dried tomatoes (finely diced)
  • 2 tbsp chopped fresh basil
  • 2 oz feta cheese

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F / 180 C.
  2. Line a 12 hole muffin tray with muffin liners, grease your muffin tray, or use a silicone muffin tray instead.
  3. Add all ingredients from whole wheat flour through to parmesan cheese in a large mixing bowl and whisk together.
  4. In a separate bowl whisk together the eggs, lemon juice, oil and oat milk. Set aside.
  5. Stir the remaining ingredients (red onion through feta cheese) through the dry ingredients.
  6. Gently fold in the wet ingredients until just combined.
  7. Use an ice cream scoop or spoon to transfer the batter to the prepared muffin tray.
  8. Bake for 25 minutes.
  9. Allow to cool completely on a cooling rack before serving.
