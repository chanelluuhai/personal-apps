Overnight Chia Oats

From MJ and Hungryman · 2025-06-09 · 5 min

Ingredients

  • 1/2 banana, mashed (optional)
  • 1/4 cup rolled oats
  • 1 tablespoon chia seeds
  • 1 tablespoon peanut butter
  • 1/2 cup milk of choice
  • 2 teaspoons honey or maple syrup (if not using banana)
  • 1/4 cup regular or Greek yogurt

Steps

  1. In a jar or container with a lid, stir together the mashed banana (if using), oats, chia seeds, peanut butter, and milk until well combined.
  2. Add the yogurt on top followed by fruit or keep the toppings separate to add in the morning—whatever works best for you!
  3. Cover and refrigerate overnight (or at least 4 hours). In the morning, give it a good stir and enjoy straight from the jar or transfer to a bowl.
