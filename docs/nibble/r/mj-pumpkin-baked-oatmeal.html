Pumpkin Baked Oatmeal

From MJ and Hungryman · 2026-09-21 · 40 min

Ingredients

  • 1 cup (240g) canned pumpkin puree
  • 1/2 cup (130g) greek yogurt
  • 3/4 cup milk of choice
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/3 cup maple syrup
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 2 cups (200g) rolled oats
  • 2 teaspoons pumpkin pie spice
  • 1/2 teaspoon cinnamon
  • 1 tablespoon chia seeds (I love this brain booster blend)
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 cup chopped walnuts or pecans

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F. Lightly grease an 8x8-inch baking dish.
  2. In the baking dish, whisk together the pumpkin, milk, Greek yogurt, eggs, vanilla, and maple syrup until well combined.
  3. Add the oats, pumpkin pie spice, cinnamon, chia seeds, baking powder, and salt. Stir until everything is evenly combined.
  4. Fold in the chopped walnuts or pecans, then spread the mixture into an even layer.
  5. Bake for 30–35 minutes, or until the center is set.
  6. Let cool for 10–15 minutes before slicing. Serve warm with a drizzle of maple syrup, a spoonful of Greek yogurt, nut or seed butter, fresh fruit, or a splash of milk. For babies, you can skip the maple syrup and serve with plain yogurt or mascarpone and soft fruit.
