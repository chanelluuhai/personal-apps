Creamy Pineapple Cucumber Smoothie

From The Picky Eater · 2026-07-14 · 5 min

Ingredients

  • 1 cup frozen diced pineapple
  • 1 medium cucumber (sliced)
  • 1 cup chopped baby spinach and kale mix
  • 1 banana (peeled and sliced)
  • 1 cup unsweetened coconut milk
  • 1 tbsp chia seeds
  • 1 lime (juiced)
  • 3 fresh mint leaves
  • ¼ cup chilled water (optional)

Steps

  1. Place all ingredients into a high speed blender.
  2. Blend until smooth, scraping down the sides as needed.
  3. Adjust thickness by adding water if needed.
  4. Serve immediately.
