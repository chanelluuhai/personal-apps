Korean radish soup

From MJ and Hungryman · 2024-08-15 · 30 min

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoons sesame oil
  • 8 ounces beef brisket or chuck, cut into bite sized pieces
  • 12 ounces Korean radish or daikon, (cut into 1 inch squares and 1/4 inch thick)
  • 7 cups water
  • 1 tablespoon minced garlic
  • 6 ounces shiitake or cremini mushroom, thinly sliced (optional)
  • 2 green onions, (sliced diagnoally)
  • 1-2 tablespoons low-sodium soy sauce for soups (see note)
  • salt to taste

Steps

  1. Heat sesame oil in a pot over medium heat. Add beef and cook until about 70% cooked.
  2. Add radish and stir for about 2-3 minutes.
  3. Add water and bring to a boil. Add garlic. Reduce heat to medium and cook for about 15-20 minutes. Using a small fine mesh strainer, skim off the foam floating on top.
  4. Add mushrooms and green onion and cook for another 5-7 minutes. Set aside portion for baby and add soy sauce You can add more to taste, if you wish.
