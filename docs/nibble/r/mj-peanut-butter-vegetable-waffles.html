Peanut Butter Waffles

From MJ and Hungryman · 2024-09-27 · 40 min

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups (235g) whole wheat flour
  • 1/4 cup (25g) rolled oats
  • 2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 1 teaspoon cinnamon
  • 1/2 cup (120g) mashed ripe banana
  • 1/3 cup (75g) creamy peanut butter
  • 2 medium eggs lightly beaten, at room temperature
  • 1 1/2 cups whole fat milk
  • 1/2 cup shredded carrots (50g measured before squeezing)
  • 1/2 cup shredded zucchini (60g measured before squeezing )

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 200 degrees Fahrenheit if you want to keep waffles warm.
  2. In a large bowl, mix all the dry ingredients until evenly distributed.
  3. In a separate bowl, combine the wet ingredients. If adding suggested vegetables, be sure to squeeze dry the zucchini and carrots before adding.
  4. Combine wet and dry ingredients together. Be careful not to over mix. Let the batter sit for 10 minutes or so.
  5. Preheat waffle iron. Grease by brushing or spraying with oil. Pour in 1/2 cup batter and cook according to manufacturer's directions until golden brown.
  6. If desired, place the waffles on a baking sheet and keep warm in the oven while you finish cooking the rest of the batter.
