Toddler Korean Bulgogi Rice Balls

From @busymamaeasybites on TikTok · 2026-09-11

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • ½ medium carrot, finely chopped
  • ½ medium onion, finely diced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • Finely diced mushrooms
  • 1 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp sesame oil
  • 2 tbsp unsweetened applesauce
  • Sprinkle of toasted sesame seeds
  • Cooked rice
  • Olive oil for cooking You can also add in any finely chopped veggies you have on hand!

Steps

  1. Sauté the garlic and onion in a little olive oil until fragrant.
  2. Add the carrots and cook until they begin to soften.
  3. Add the ground beef and cook through, breaking it into small pieces. I used a paper towel to carefully dab away any excess fat.
  4. Add the finely diced mushrooms or any other finely chopped veggies you’d like.
  5. Stir in the low-sodium soy sauce, sesame oil and applesauce.
  6. Continue cooking until everything is fully cooked and combined, then sprinkle with toasted sesame seeds.
  7. Mix the bulgogi beef with cooked rice.
  8. Once cool enough to handle, use your hands to form into toddler-sized rice balls. That’s it!
