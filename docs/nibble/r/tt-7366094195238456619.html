Joomukbap is a Korean style rice ball

From @mini.tiger.zen on TikTok · 2024-05-07

Ingredients

  • 1 cup of cooked ground meat + seasoning (I used ground beef, and added 1 tablespoon of soy sauce), for younger babies, skip seasoning.
  • 1 cup of cooked, chopped vegetables (I recommend carrots, broccoli and mushroom — less moisture, easier to shape)

Steps

  1. Mix cooked rice, cooked meat, and vegetables in a bowl * Add and mix in 1 tablespoon of Sesame oil * Shape the mixture into balls!
  2. Optional: shred roasted seaweed in a bowl, roll the rice ball and cover it with the seaweed Feel free to season as desired for older toddlers, kids & adults.
  3. Adding 1-3 tablespoons of soy sauce will enhance flavor.
