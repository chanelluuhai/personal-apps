4 Ingredient Cilantro Lime Rice

From The Picky Eater · 2026-07-23 · 35 min

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked rice (brown, basmati, or white rice, use frozen rice for fast and easy option)
  • 4 cups water
  • ¼ cup chopped cilantro
  • 3 - 4 teaspoons fresh lime juice
  • ¼ - ¾ teaspoon sea salt (see note)

Steps

  1. Combine rice, water, and lime juice in your rice cooker, and cook following your rice cooker's instructions.
  2. Once cooked, remove lid and mix in sea salt and chopped cilantro.
  3. Serve alone or as part of a meal!
