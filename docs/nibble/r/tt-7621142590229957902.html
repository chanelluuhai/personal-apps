One meal for the whole family

From @soniamazri on TikTok · 2026-03-25 · 60 min

Ingredients

  • 1 lb lamb
  • 1 small onion
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 4 oz mushrooms
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • ½ tsp oregano
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • 1 dash cinnamon
  • Salt (skip for babies under 1)
  • ½ cup water

Steps

  1. Brown lamb in butter and olive oil.
  2. Add onion, garlic, and spices.
  3. Cover and cook 10 minutes.
  4. Add water and slow cook for 1 hour until tender.
  5. Sauté mushrooms separately and mix in at the end.
  6. Serve with rice or mashed potatoes.
