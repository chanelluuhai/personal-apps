Coconut Milkshake

From The Picky Eater · 2026-06-12 · 5 min

Ingredients

  • 2 cups coconut cream (from the can)
  • 1 cup vanilla ice cream (or vegan vanilla ice cream)
  • 1 cup unsweetened coconut milk (or any milk)
  • 4 ice cubes (optional)
  • 2 tsp sugar (optional)

Steps

  1. Add all ingredients except the sugar, to a high-speed blender and blend until smooth.
  2. Adjust the sweetness by adding sugar if needed. If you add sugar make sure to blend for 1-2 minutes to incorporate it into the milkshake.
  3. Adjust the consistency by adding more coconut milk if needed.
  4. Serve immediately. To make it an even more decadent dessert, add a scoop of ice cream on top!
