Sardine Pancakes

From MJ and Hungryman · 2026-10-09 · 10 min

Ingredients

  • 2 sardine fillets
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tablespoons mashed sweet potato
  • 1 tablespoon flour (any kind)
  • 1 teaspoon ground flaxseeds

Steps

  1. Add the sardines to a bowl and mash thoroughly with a fork until mostly smooth. Add the egg, mashed sweet potato, flour, and ground flaxseed and stir until everything is well combined.
  2. Heat a lightly oiled skillet over medium heat. Scoop the batter into the pan to form 3–4 small pancakes, gently flattening them as needed.
  3. Cook for about 2–3 minutes per side, or until the pancakes are lightly golden and the egg is cooked through. Transfer to a plate and allow the pancakes to cool before serving to your baby or toddler.
