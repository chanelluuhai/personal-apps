Soft chickpea heart cookies

From @toddler.snack.therapy on Instagram · 2026-08-05 · 20 min

Ingredients

  • 1 cup (160g) cooked chickpeas
  • 1 1/4 cup (150g) whole wheat flour
  • 2 tbsp softened butter
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 egg
  • 1/4 tsp cinnamon
  • 1/4 tsp baking powder

Steps

  1. Blend chickpeas, butter, honey, and egg until smooth.
  2. Add flour, cinnamon, and baking powder; mix into a soft dough.
  3. Form dough into small balls, flatten slightly.
  4. Press a small heart in the center with your fingertip.
  5. Bake at 175°C (350°F) for 20 minutes, golden and soft.
  6. Fill the hearts with homemade fiber rich jam once cooled.
  7. Tip: Store in an airtight container for up to 3 days.
