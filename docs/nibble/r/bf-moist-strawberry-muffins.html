Kid-Requested Healthy Strawberry Yogurt Muffins

From Baby Foode · 2023-07-25 · 25 min

Ingredients

  • 1 3/4 cups white whole wheat flour (see notes)
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp baking soda
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 1/2 cup pure maple syrup, (honey (babies over 1) or agave nectar)
  • 1/3 cup melted butter or oil (such as olive oil, avocado oil, vegetable oil or coconut oil)
  • 1/3 cup milk, (regular or plain plant-based)
  • 1/3 cup plain whole milk yogurt
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 cup strawberries, (chopped)
  • 1/4 cup full-fat cream cheese
  • 3 tbsp pure maple syrup
  • 3 tbsp freeze-dried strawberries, (crushed)
  • 3 tbsp milk (optional)

Steps

  1. Prep: Preheat the oven to 375° F. Spray or line a muffin tin.
  2. Whisk: In a medium bowl, whisk together the flour, baking soda, baking powder, salt, and cinnamon.
  3. Stir: In a large mixing bowl, add in the maple syrup, butter/oil, eggs, milk, yogurt ,and vanilla extract and mix until smooth.
  4. Combine: Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients and stir until just combined. Do not overmix.
  5. Add Strawberries: Gently add the strawberries to the muffin batter and then fold them into the batter.
  6. Fill Muffin Tin: Scoop the batter into the muffin tin 3/4 of the way full.
  7. Bake: Place the muffin tin into the oven, and bake for 18-20 minutes or until just golden brown.
  8. Make Drizzle: Meanwhile, if using the cream cheese drizzle, place the cream cheese in a microwave-safe bowl and microwave for 20-30 seconds until just warm. Add in the maple syrup and mix until creamy and smooth. Add in the crushed freeze-dried strawberries and stir. If the drizzle is too thick, add 1-3 tbsp milk. Set aside.
  9. Eat: Let cool to the touch, then drizzle with the cream cheese drizzle (optional). If the cream cheese drizzle is too thick, you can heat it in the microwave for 10-15 seconds. Serve and enjoy!
