Broccoli Cheddar Orzo

From @soniamazri on TikTok · 2026-08-28

Ingredients

  • Chicken
  • 1 chicken breast
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • ½ tsp onion powder
  • ½ tsp garlic powder
  • ½ tsp oregano
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • Salt (1 year+ only)
  • Black pepper Orzo
  • 1½ cups orzo
  • 3 cups water or chicken broth
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 head broccoli
  • ½ tsp garlic powder
  • ½ tsp onion powder
  • ½ tsp paprika
  • ½ tsp turmeric
  • Salt (1 year+ only)
  • Black pepper
  • ½ cup heavy cream
  • 1 cup cheddar cheese

Steps

  1. Season the chicken with the olive oil, onion powder, garlic powder, oregano, paprika, pepper, and salt if serving 1 year+, then cook until golden.
  2. In the same pan, cook the garlic in butter, then add the orzo, broccoli, seasonings, and boiling water or broth.
  3. Cook until tender, stir in the heavy cream and cheddar cheese, then return the chicken to the pan
