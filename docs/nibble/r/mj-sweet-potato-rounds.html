Best Sweet Potato Slices

From MJ and Hungryman · 2024-10-10 · 35 min

Ingredients

  • 2 medium sweet potatoes
  • 2 tablespoons oil of choice (avocado, olive, or coconut oil)
  • Optional Seasonings: cinnamon, cumin, Smoked paprika, chili powder, curry powder, oregano, etc.

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 400° Fahrenheit.
  2. Prep the sweet potatoes - scrub really well to remove any dirt and bacteria. Dry throughly with a kitchen towel. Using a sharp knife, slice into 1/2 inch rounds. Drizzle oil and make sure all the round slices are coated well to ensure a crispy exterior and a soft interior.
  3. Arrange the rounds in single layer on the prepared baking pan. Bake for 25-30 minutes, flipping halfway, until golden brown.
  4. Enjoy as is or add whatever toppings you wish.
