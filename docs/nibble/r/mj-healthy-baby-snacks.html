Best Healthy Baby Snacks

From MJ and Hungryman · 2024-12-23 · 5 min

Ingredients

  • 1 tablespoon Cooked salmon or sardines (fresh or canned)
  • 3 tablespoon Edamame - Flatten with your fingers to split in half.
  • 1 tablespoon Ground beef, chicken, or turkey crumbles
  • 1 Lentil balls
  • 1 strip Tofu
  • 1/4 cup Unsweetened applesauce
  • 1-2 Avocado spears rolled in hemp seeds
  • Soft cooked apple
  • Ripe mango spear rolled in flax seeds
  • Flatted blueberries
  • 1/4 cup Pear hummus
  • 2 Sweet Potato Rounds
  • 2 broccoli florets
  • tomatoes
  • Vegetable omelette
  • Butternut squash strip
  • Carrot spear
  • cabbage strip
  • 1/4 cup plain yogurt
  • 1/4 cup Dry cereals
  • 1/4 cup Baby puffs
  • Fruit or Vegetable Pouches
  • 1/4 cup Freeze-dried fruit

Steps

  1. If between 9-12 months and your child IS showing interest in more food in addition to their 3 meals and milk, then you can try incorporating no more than 1 snack per day to help tide them over until the next eating opportunity.
  2. Aim to include at least 2 food groups. If unable, make sure the one food that you do offer is an iron-rich food.
  3. Finally, start with just a small amount and offer more if your baby seems interested. This will help reduce overwhelm and minimize food waste.
