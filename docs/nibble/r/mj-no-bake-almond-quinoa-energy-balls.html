Almond Butter Protein Balls

From MJ and Hungryman · 2024-11-06 · 45 min

Ingredients

  • 1 cup cooked quinoa (cooled)
  • 1/2 cup rolled oats (50g)
  • 1/2 cup almond butter
  • 1/4 cup honey or maple syrup
  • 2 tablespoon ground flaxseeds (or chia seeds)
  • 1/4 cup unsweetened shredded coconut
  • 1/4 cup dried fruit or chocolate chips
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract (optional)
  • 1/2 teaspoon cinnamon

Steps

  1. In a large bowl, add all the ingredients. Mix well using a spatula or your hands until evenly combined. Refrigerate for at least 30 minutes to firm up.
  2. Scoop about 1-2 tablespoons of the mixture and roll into small balls using slightly damp or wet hands.
  3. Store in an airtight container in the fridge for up to a week or in the freezer for up to 3 months.
