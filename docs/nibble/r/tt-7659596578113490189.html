Don’t throw away those leftover sandwich crusts

From @busymamaeasybites on TikTok · 2026-07-07

Ingredients

  • Leftover sandwich crusts
  • 1 egg
  • 1/4 cup milk
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
  • Dash of cinnamon
  • Unsalted butter for the pan

Steps

  1. Roll each crust into a spiral and secure it with a long toothpick.
  2. Dip into the egg mixture and let it soak it up.
  3. Cook in a buttered pan over low-medium heat for a few minutes on each side until golden brown.
  4. Serve with maple syrup or yogurt for dipping!
  5. Such a fun way to reduce food waste while making a toddler-approved breakfast or snack!
