Apple Cinnamon Baked Oatmeal

From MJ and Hungryman · 2024-10-02 · 30 min

Ingredients

  • 2 cups rolled oats (200g)
  • 1/2 cup unsweetened applesauce (110g) OR 1 medium banana, mashed
  • 1 tablespoon chia seeds
  • 1 teaspoon cinnamon
  • 2-3 tablespoons maple syrup (optional), see note
  • 2 cups milk of choice
  • 1 cup apples (120g), diced or shredded

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Lightly grease a 8 inch square pan.
  2. Add all the ingredients and stir to combine. Add the apples on top.
  3. Bake for 25-30 minutes.
