Banana Yogurt

From MJ and Hungryman · 2024-07-24 · 5 min

Ingredients

  • 1 cup plain yogurt
  • 1 medium ripe banana
  • 1/2 teaspoon cinnamon
  • 1-2 tablespoons Peanut butter, nut butter, or sunflower seed butter
  • 1-2 tablespoons honey or maple syrup

Steps

  1. Add banana to a blender and puree until smooth.
  2. In a bowl, combine the plain yogurt with the banana puree. Stir in any of the optional add-ins.
