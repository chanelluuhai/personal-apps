Deviled eggs but make them healthy and no mayo

From @pickyeaterblog on TikTok · 2025-04-23 · 10 min

Ingredients

  • eggs
  • greek yogurt
  • garlic
  • onion
  • salt
  • pepper

Steps

  1. Did you know if you boil eggs for 10 minutes, cool in an ice bath, peel, slice in half, scoop out those yolks, mix yolks with greek yogurt, mustard, garlic and onion powder, salt and pepper and chopped dill, mash till creamy, then pipe back into egg whites, top with paprika, dill and chill.
