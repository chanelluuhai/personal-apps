Banana Date Shake

From Weelicious · 2009-02-09 · 5 min

Ingredients

  • 2 frozen bananas
  • 1 cup milk (any type will do)
  • 3 dates, pitted
  • 1/4 cup orange juice

Steps

  1. Place all the ingredients in a blender in order given.
  2. Puree on high until smooth and creamy.
