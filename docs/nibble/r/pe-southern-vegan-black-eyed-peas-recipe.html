Southern Vegan Black Eyed Peas Recipe

From The Picky Eater · 2026-09-29 · 50 min

Ingredients

  • 1 cup sweet or yellow onion (, chopped)
  • 3 cloves garlic (, chopped)
  • 1 teaspoon sea salt
  • Drizzle of oil or broth to sauté
  • 1 cup celery (, chopped)
  • 1 cup red pepper (, chopped)
  • 1 jalapeno (, seeded and chopped)
  • 1 teaspoon fresh thyme
  • ¼ teaspoon cayenne pepper (, optional for added spice)
  • ¼ teaspoon liquid smoke (, see note)
  • 1 cup dried black eyed peas (, soaked overnight (see note)
  • 3 cups veggie broth (, low sodium if needed)
  • 4 cups cooked white or brown rice (I love the frozen cooked to make it fast)
  • 1 bunch chard (slice and sauté (see note)
  • Sliced green onions (garnish)

Steps

  1. Sauté the onion and salt in oil/broth until the onion is soft. About 5 minutes.
  2. Add in garlic and saute for 1-2 minutes until fragrant.
  3. Add the celery, red pepper, jalapeno, thyme, cayenne and liquid smoke. Sauté about 3 more minutes until the celery and pepper are just beginning to soften
  4. Add in the black eyes and broth. Simmer covered for 25-30 minutes until beans are soft. Uncover and cook 5 or so minutes until all the liquid has evaporated.
  5. Serve with rice and chard and garnish with green onions!
