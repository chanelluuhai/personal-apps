Healthy Pumpkin Oatmeal Bars

From MJ and Hungryman · 2024-09-18 · 35 min

Ingredients

  • 2 cups (200g) rolled oats
  • 1 cup (200g) cooked red lentils (or swap with 1/2 cup oats)
  • 2 teaspoons pumpkin pie spice
  • 1 teaspoon cinnamon
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 2 tablespoons ground flaxseeds (or 2 eggs)
  • 1 cup (230g) canned pumpkin
  • 1 cup whole fat milk
  • 1/4 cup (55g) unsweetened applesauce
  • 1/4 cup maple syrup
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 3-4 soft medjool dates
  • 1/3 cup (40g) finely chopped pecans
  • 1/3-1/2 cup chocolate chips

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees Fahrenheit. Line a 9x9 inch baking pan with parchment paper for easy removal. Set aside.
  2. Pulse the oats in a food processor/blender until it turns into flour. Pour into a large bowl and combine with pumpkin pie spice, cinnamon, baking powder, and ground flaxseeds.
  3. Add cooked lentils (cooled), pumpkin, milk, applesauce, and vanilla extract into the food processor/blender and process until smooth.
  4. Pour into the bowl with the oatmeal mixture. Do not overmix. Fold in pecans and chocolate chips if using.
  5. Pour the batter into baking pan. Bake 20-25 minutes until the center is set and the inserted toothpick comes out clean.
  6. Allow for it cool in the pan for about 10 minutes before transferring to a cooling rack. COOL COMPLETELY before slicing.
