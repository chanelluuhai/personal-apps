Mini PIzza

From MJ and Hungryman · 2025-03-17 · 20 min

Ingredients

  • 12 naan dippers, or any pizza dough or crust of choice
  • 1/3 cup pizza sauce, see note
  • 1 cup shredded mozzarella cheese
  • Toppings of choice, see note

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 425 degrees F. Place pizza crust on a lined baking tray. If using pizza dough, roll it out and cut into small circles using a small round cookie cutter or the rim of a cup.
  2. Top with pizza sauce and cheese.Add the toppings, pressing gently in, and bake until cheese has melted and the crust is lightly browned, about 8-10 minutes.
