Cottage cheese protein wraps

From MJ and Hungryman · 2025-04-21 · 25 min

Ingredients

  • 1 1/4 cup self-rising flour (180g)
  • 3/4 cup full fat cottage cheese (180g)
  • warm water, as needed
  • butter, for greasing the pan
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder, onion powder, Italian seasoning, any seasonings you like

Steps

  1. In a medium bowl, combine cottage cheese, self-rising flour, seasonings and salt (if using). Stir with a spoon until everything comes together into a rough dough.
  2. Lightly flour your hands and shape the dough into one big ball. Then cut it into 4 equal pieces.
  3. On a lightly floured surface, use a rolling pin to roll each piece into a thin circle.
  4. Heat a non-stick skillet over medium heat and add a small pat of butter. Once melted, place a rolled-out wrap in the pan. Cook for 2-3 minutes per side, until golden brown spots appear and the wrap is cooked through. Add more butter as needed between wraps.
