Vegan Patatas Bravas

From The Picky Eater · 2026-07-14 · 55 min

Ingredients

  • 2 pounds russet potatoes (, cubed)
  • 1 drizzle oil or broth (, to get the coating to stick (see note)
  • ¼ cup cornstarch
  • ¼ cup cornmeal
  • ½-¾ teaspoon sea salt
  • ¼ teaspoon ground black pepper
  • parsley garnish (, optional)
  • garlic aioli (, optional)
  • 1 cup veggie broth (, low sodium if needed)
  • ¾ cup raw cashews (, see note)
  • 1 tablespoon apple cider vinegar
  • 2 teaspoons hot sauce
  • 2 teaspoons paprika
  • 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • ½-¾ teaspoon sea salt

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 450 degrees F / 230 C
  2. Parboil the cubed potatoes. Put them in a pot and cover them with water. Add a pinch of salt.
  3. Cover and bring to a boil over high heat. About 5-7 minutes.
  4. Uncover and cook at a rolling boil for 5 more minutes until slightly soft.
  5. Drain and rinse with cold water.
  6. Dab dry with a paper towel and put into a bowl.
  7. Mix the cornstarch, cornmeal, salt and pepper in a bowl until combined. Add to the bowl with the potatoes.
  8. Drizzle on the oil or broth and mix well so they are covered.
  9. Spread out on a parchment lined baking sheet.
  10. Bake covered for 30-35 minutes, until potatoes are golden and soft.
  11. Meanwhile make the paprika sauce by putting everything into a blender and blending until smooth and creamy.
  12. Serve with sauces!
