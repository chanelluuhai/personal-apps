Vegan Pumpkin Pancakes

From Weelicious · 2008-11-27 · 13 min

Ingredients

  • 1 cup all purpose flour
  • 1/2 cup whole wheat flour
  • 2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1/2 teaspoon cinnamon
  • 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt
  • 1 1/4 cups almond, oat, soy or other plant based milk of choice
  • 1 tablespoon maple syrup, plus more for serving
  • 1 tablespoon vegetable oil, plus more for greasing
  • 1/2 cup pumpkin puree

Steps

  1. Mix the dry ingredients in a bowl.
  2. Mix the wet ingredients in a separate bowl.
  3. Add the wet ingredients to the dry ingredients and whisk to combine (don’t over mix, some lumps are fine).
  4. Heat a griddle or large saute pan over medium heat and lightly
  5. grease with oil.
  6. Ladle as many pancakes as possible (about 2 tbsp of batter for each one) on the griddle and cook 2 minutes or until bubbles start forming on the surface and the bottom is golden brown.
  7. Flip the pancakes and cook another 2 minutes.
  8. Repeat the process until you run out of batter (you can hold the finished pancakes in a 250 degree oven while you finish making the pancakes).
  9. Serve with maple syrup and enjoy.
