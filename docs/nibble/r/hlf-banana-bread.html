Healthy Banana Bread

From Healthy Little Foodies · 2020-03-19 · 70 min

Ingredients

  • 4 Medjool Dates, pitted
  • 125ml (½ cup) Milk
  • 80ml (⅓ cup) Coconut Oil
  • 60g (¼ cup) Natural Greek Yoghurt
  • 2 Eggs
  • 1 tsp Vanilla Extract
  • 250g (1 cup) (around 2 large/3 medium small) Ripe Bananas, mashed
  • 215g (1¾ cup) White Wholemeal (wholewheat) Flour
  • 1 tsp Cinnamon
  • 1 tsp Baking Soda

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 160ºC (320ºF) and grease a loaf pan.
  2. Add the Medjool dates to a food processor and blend until it forms a paste. Add the milk and continue to blend until mixed.
  3. In a large mixing bowl add the date mixture, oil, yoghurt, eggs and vanilla. Whisk until combined. Add the mashed banana and stir.
  4. In a seperate bowl, mix together the flour, baking soda and cinnamon.
  5. Add the dry ingredients to the wet and stir until just combined. Be careful not to overmix, some lumps are good.
  6. Pour the batter into the prepared loaf pan and bake for 55 - 60 mins. Check the loaf is ready by inserting a skewer into the centre. If the stick comes out clean, or with a few crumbles on it, the bread is done.
  7. Allow to cool in the pan for around 10 mins and then transfer to a wire rack to cool for a further 20 mins + before slicing.
