Halloween Fruit Tray

From The Picky Eater · 2026-10-29 · 10 min

Ingredients

  • 12 clementines (peeled and separated)
  • 1 cup red grapes (washed and picked off the vine)
  • 1/4 cup blueberries
  • 4 strawberries (halved)
  • 4 slices granny smith apple

Steps

  1. Peel clementines and separate them into wedges. Cover tray completely with wedges.
  2. Slice strawberries in half. Arrange into a smile.
  3. Place washed blueberries into the center. Arrange into a circle or triangle for the nose.
  4. Wash grapes. Arrange into two equal circles to make the eyes.
  5. Slice green apple into wedges. Arrange at the top to create the pumpkin stem.
