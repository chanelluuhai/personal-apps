Veggie pizza bites + Greek yogurt + kiwi

From @soniamazri on TikTok · 2026-05-25 · 25 min

Ingredients

  • 1 small zucchini
  • ½ potato
  • 1 small carrot
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tbsp flour Topping
  • Tomato sauce
  • Mozzarella cheese

Steps

  1. Bake 25 min at 400°F
  2. Add sauce + cheese
  3. Bake 5 more min
