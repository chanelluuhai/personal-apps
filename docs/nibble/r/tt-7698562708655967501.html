Ingredients

  • 300 g ground beef (or half beef / half lamb)
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 3 garlic clove, minced
  • 150 g tomato purée or crushed tomatoes
  • 3 tsp tomato concentrate (paste)
  • 1 pinch of nutmeg (optional)
  • 3 tablespoons of each oregano and paprika Fresh parsley added before finishing For the béchamel
  • 3 tablespoons butter
  • 2.5 tablespoons flour

Steps

  1. Cut the potatoes into slices about 0.5 cm thick.
  2. Spread them on a baking sheet, brush with olive oil and bake at 350 for 20-30 minutes until golden brown.
  3. Layer and bake at 350 for 30 minutes!
