Greek Chicken Bake

From MJ and Hungryman · 2026-04-13 · 55 min

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 pounds boneless, skinless chicken thighs, diced
  • 1 ½ cups orzo (270g)
  • 1/2 red onion, thinly sliced
  • 2 1/2 cups chicken broth
  • 1 tablespoon minced garlic
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1/2 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • juice of 1 lemon
  • 1 red bell pepper, chopped (½-inch pieces)
  • 1 medium, zucchini thinly sliced
  • 1 cup favorite tzatziki sauce
  • chopped parsley, extra lemon juice, feta cheese, cucumber

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F.
  2. In a large casserole dish, add the orzo, diced chicken, and sliced red onion.
  3. In a bowl, whisk together the broth, garlic, oregano, smoked paprika, and lemon juice. Pour over everything and give it a gentle stir to combine.
  4. Scatter the red bell peppers on top (don’t mix—this helps them roast slightly instead of getting too soft).
  5. Cover tightly (I like to use a baking mat instead of foil) and bake for 25–30 minutes, until the orzo is tender and most of the liquid is absorbed.
  6. Remove from the oven, stir, then mix in the tzatziki sauce and zucchini.
  7. Return to the oven uncovered and bake for another 10 minutes, until the zucchini is tender and everything is creamy and cozy.
  8. Finish with fresh parsley and a squeeze of lemon juice before serving. You can set aside portion for your baby and add more tzatziki sauce, feta, olives...whatever you're in the mood for!
