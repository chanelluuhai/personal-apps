Strawberry Popsicles

From MJ and Hungryman · 2024-06-21 · 365 min

Ingredients

  • 1 pound fresh ripe strawberries, hulled and sliced
  • 3/4 cup plain Greek yogurt
  • 2-4 tablespoons honey (see note)
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract (optional)

Steps

  1. Add all the ingredients into a blender and blend until smooth.
  2. Pour the mixture into your mold. Freeze for at least six hours, preferably overnight.
