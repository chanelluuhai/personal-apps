Trader Joe's Channa Masala Recipe

From The Picky Eater · 2026-07-27 · 15 min

Ingredients

  • 1 box Trader Joe's Frozen Channa Masala
  • 15 oz canned chickpeas (1 can)
  • ½ cup water (more or less depending on how thick you like your channa masala)
  • ¼ red onion (diced)
  • 1-2 tomatoes (diced)
  • cilantro (chopped, as a garnish)
  • ½ tsp salt (more or less to taste)

Steps

  1. Heat the frozen Channa Masala box halfway (it says 4 minutes on the box, so heat in the microwave for 2 minutes)
  2. Combine the defrosted Channa Masala box, the garbanzo beans and 1/2 cup water in a medium pot over medium-high heat. Cook 1-2 minutes.
  3. Bring to a boil and cook 1 minute. Then reduce heat to low and simmer for 10 minutes (and up to 1 hour). Add more water as needed (to achieve the consistency you want).
  4. Mash some of the garbanzo beans to thicken the curry.
  5. Serve warm (top with diced onions, diced tomatoes, cilantro to taste)
