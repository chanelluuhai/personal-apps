The viral brothy beef bowl… but make it toddler-friendly

From @soniamazri on TikTok · 2026-10-01 · 5 min

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 cup grated carrot
  • ½ tsp garlic powder
  • ½ tsp onion powder
  • ½ tsp paprika
  • Black pepper, optional Creamy broth
  • 2 cups Beef Bone Broth
  • 1 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 garlic clove, minced
  • ¼ cup heavy cream
  • ¼ cup finely shredded cheddar
  • ½ tsp Italian seasoning To serve: cooked rice + optional parsley

Steps

  1. Brown the ground beef and grated carrot with the seasonings until cooked through and golden.
  2. Sauté garlic for 30 seconds, then add tomato paste and cook for 1–2 minutes.
  3. Pour in the Beef Bone Broth, add Italian seasoning, and simmer for 5 minutes.
  4. Lower the heat and stir in the heavy cream and cheddar until creamy.
  5. Add rice to your bowl, top with the beef, and ladle that creamy broth over everything.
