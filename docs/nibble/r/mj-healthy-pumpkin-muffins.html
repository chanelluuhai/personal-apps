Healthy Pumpkin Muffins

From MJ and Hungryman · 2025-09-09 · 30 min

Ingredients

  • 1 cup canned pumpkin (240g)
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/3 cup maple syrup
  • 1/4 cup unsalted butter, melted
  • 1/2 cup whole milk (or non-dairy alternative)
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1 3/4 cups white whole wheat flour (215g)
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 2 teaspoons pumpkin pie spice
  • 3/4 cup semi-sweet chocolate chips (optional)

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C) and lightly grease or line a 12-cup muffin pan.
  2. In a large bowl, whisk together the pumpkin puree, eggs, maple syrup, melted butter, milk, and vanilla until smooth.
  3. In a separate bowl, stir together the flour, baking powder, baking soda, salt, and pumpkin pie spice.
  4. Add the dry ingredients to the wet ingredients and gently stir until just combined. Do not overmix! If using, fold in the chocolate chips (or dried fruit/nuts).
  5. Divide the batter evenly among the muffin cups, filling each about ¾ full. Bake for 18–22 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.
  6. Let cool in the pan for 5 minutes, then transfer to a wire rack to cool completely.
