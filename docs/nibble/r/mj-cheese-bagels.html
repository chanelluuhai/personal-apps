Cheese Bagels

From MJ and Hungryman · 2026-06-01 · 29 min

Ingredients

  • 1 cup (120g) self rising flour
  • 1 cup (240g) plain Greek yogurt (full fat preferred)
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 3/4 cup shredded cheddar cheese
  • 1/2 cup shredded carrots (optional, but adds a great veggie boost)
  • 1 egg (for egg wash)

Steps

  1. Preheat your oven to 375°F. Line a baking sheet with parchment paper or use a nonstick baking sheet like mine (linked above).
  2. In a bowl, combine the self-rising flour, Greek yogurt, and garlic powder. Mix until it comes together into a shaggy dough.
  3. Add in the shredded cheese and carrots, then gently knead everything together on a lightly floured surface until you have a soft dough. Try not to overwork it. Just enough to bring it together.
  4. Divide the dough into 4 equal pieces. You can shape them two ways: Rope method: Roll each piece into a rope (about 6–8 inches long), then bring the ends together to form a circle and pinch to seal.Hole method (easier!): Roll into a ball, then use your finger to poke a hole through the center and gently stretch it out.
  5. Place the bagels on your prepared baking sheet. Brush the tops with beaten egg for that golden finish.
  6. Bake for 20–25 minutes, until puffed and golden brown. Let them cool for about 10 minutes before slicing (if you can wait that long!).
