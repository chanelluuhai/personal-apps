Peanut Butter Muffins

From The Picky Eater · 2026-06-24 · 30 min

Ingredients

  • 2 cups whole wheat flour
  • 1 cup coconut sugar
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • ¼ tsp salt
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup unsweetened almond milk
  • ⅔ cup peanut butter
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • ¾ cup dark chocolate chips (optional)

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 340 degrees F / 170 C. Line a muffin tray with muffin liners and set aside.
  2. In a medium mixing bowl whisk together the flour, coconut sugar, baking powder and salt until well combined. If using chocolate chips, stir through the dry ingredients.
  3. In a separate mixing bowl, whisk together the eggs, milk, peanut butter and vanilla extract until smooth.
  4. Gently fold the wet ingredients through the dry ingredients. Do not over mix.
  5. Evenly divide the batter amongst the prepared muffin liners.
  6. Bake for 20 minutes or until an inserted toothpick comes out clean.
  7. Allow to cool on a cooling rack.
  8. Store in an airtight container in a cool dry place.
