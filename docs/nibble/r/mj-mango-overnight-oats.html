Mango Overnight Oats

From MJ and Hungryman · 2025-05-03 · 250 min

Ingredients

  • 1 cup (200g) fresh or frozen mangoes
  • 12 ounce can full-fat unsweetened coconut milk
  • 1/2 cup yogurt, plain or Greek-style
  • 2 dates (optional)
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 2 cups rolled oats (1/2 cup per jar)
  • 4 teaspoons chia seeds (1 teaspoon per jar)

Steps

  1. Make the mango coconut blend: In a blender, combine the mango, coconut milk, yogurt, dates, and vanilla. Blend until smooth and creamy.
  2. Assemble the jars: Place ½ cup rolled oats and 1 teaspoon chia seeds into each jar or container. Pour the mango coconut mixture evenly over the oats. Stir well to make sure everything’s combined. Let it sit for about 5 minutes then stir again.
  3. Chill overnight: Cover and refrigerate the jars for at least 4 hours or overnight. The oats and chia seeds will soak up all that tropical goodness and turn into a creamy, pudding-like texture.
  4. Serve and enjoy: Give the oats a good stir before eating. Add a splash of milk to loosen it up, if needed. You can top with more fresh mango, shredded coconut, or a sprinkle of granola if you want a little crunch!
