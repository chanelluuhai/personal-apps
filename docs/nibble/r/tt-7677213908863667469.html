Ground Beef Stew Over Rice

From @soniamazri on TikTok · 2026-08-30

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 small onion
  • 2 cups peas & carrots
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • ½ cup tomato sauce
  • 2 cups water
  • ½ tsp paprika
  • ½ tsp garlic powder
  • ½ tsp onion powder
  • ½ tsp oregano
  • Salt (1 year+ only)
  • Black pepper
  • Fresh parsley
  • Cooked rice

Steps

  1. Brown the ground beef in olive oil, then cook the onion until softened.
  2. Add the peas & carrots, garlic, tomato sauce, seasonings, and water.
  3. Simmer until the vegetables are tender, stir in fresh parsley, and serve over warm rice
