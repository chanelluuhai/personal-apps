Boiled Green Beans Recipe

From The Picky Eater · 2026-06-30 · 20 min

Ingredients

  • 1 lb fresh green beans (washed and ends trimmed)
  • 2 tsp kosher salt
  • 1 tbsp olive oil

Steps

  1. Bring a large pot of water to a boil.
  2. Add 1 teaspoon kosher salt and the green beans. Bring to a boil and cook, uncovered, until just tender, about 5 minutes.
  3. Add the beans to a colander, shake and drain.
  4. Season with the remaining 1 teaspoon of salt and 1 tablespoon of butter or olive oil. Serve immediately.
