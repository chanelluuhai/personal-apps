Vegetarian Fajita Bowl

From The Picky Eater · 2026-06-24 · 35 min

Ingredients

  • 1 tbsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp coriander, ground
  • 1 tsp cumin, ground
  • ¼ tsp cayenne pepper
  • ½ tsp garlic powder
  • ½ tsp onion powder
  • ½ tsp oregano, dried
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 2 red bell peppers, sliced
  • 1 yellow bell pepper, sliced
  • 1 red onion
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 cup frozen corn kernels
  • 1 cup brown rice, cooked
  • 1 tin black beans, drained and washed
  • 1 avocado
  • 2 limes
  • ½ bunch fresh cilantro
  • 2 jalapenos, sliced

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F / 180 C.
  2. Add all seasoning ingredients into a small mixing bowl and mix together.
  3. Add the sliced bell peppers and onion into a mixing bowl along with the oil and half of the seasoning mixture, stir to coat.
  4. Spread the coated vegetables onto a baking tray and cook in the oven for 10-15 minutes.
  5. While the bell peppers are cooking, heat the frozen corn and black beans in a small skillet until hot, sprinkle the remaining seasoning and stir to coat.
  6. Heat the cooked rice and evenly divide the rice and the corn mixture between 3-4 bowls.
  7. Top with the cooked bell peppers, sliced avocado, lime wedges, fresh cilantro, and sliced jalapeno.
