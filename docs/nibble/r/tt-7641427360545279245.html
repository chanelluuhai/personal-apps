These easy sweet potato nuggets are perfect for snack time or a quick meal

From @busymamaeasybites on TikTok · 2026-05-19 · 35 min

Ingredients

  • 1 large sweet potato
  • 1/2 cup frozen peas
  • Handful of shredded cheese
  • Optional: garlic powder

Steps

  1. Bake at 350°F for 35 minutes, flipping halfway through and adding a little more cheese on top when flipping
