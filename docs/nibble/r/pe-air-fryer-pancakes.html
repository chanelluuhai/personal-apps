Quick & Fluffy Air Fryer Pancakes (No Flipping!)

From The Picky Eater · 2026-06-09 · 18 min

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 4 tsp baking powder
  • ¼ tsp baking soda
  • ¼ cup sugar
  • 2 cups low fat milk
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • ¼ cup butter, melted

Steps

  1. Preheat the air fryer to 330 degrees F / 170 C while preparing the pancake batter.
  2. Sift the flour, baking powder and soda into a large mixing bowl.
  3. Add the sugar and whisk together. Set aside.
  4. Whisk together the milk, egg, vanilla and butter.
  5. Pour the milk mixture into the flour mixture and whisk until smooth.
  6. Lightly spray four 4-inch round springform tins with oil. Scoop ½ cup batter into each tin and place on the airfryer tray.
  7. Cook in the air fryer for 10-12 minutes. Allow the pancakes to rest in the tin for 2-3 minutes before removing and cooling on a cooling rack.
  8. Repeat with remaining batter. Batter makes 10 pancakes.
  9. Serve pancakes topped with a drizzle of maple syrup and fresh berries.
