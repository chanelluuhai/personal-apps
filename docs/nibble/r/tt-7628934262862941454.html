Spinach French toast

From @soniamazri on TikTok · 2026-04-15

Ingredients

  • 2 slices whole grain bread
  • 1 egg
  • Splash of milk
  • Handful spinach
  • 1 tbsp mozzarella
  • 1 tbsp cheddar
  • Butter

Steps

  1. Blend everything
  2. Dip bread
  3. Cook in butter until golden. A simple way to serve something balanced without overthinking
