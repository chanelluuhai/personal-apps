Healthy Beet Muffins for Babies

From MJ and Hungryman · 2023-02-06 · 25 min

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup (50g) rolled oats
  • 3 small or 1 to 1 1/2 medium (170g) cooked beetroot
  • 3/4 cup (120g) no-salt-added chickpeas, rinsed and drained
  • 1/2 cup (125g) smooth peanut butter, (at room temperature)
  • 1 1/2 medium (170g) ripe banana
  • 1 teaspoons baking powder, aluminum-free
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1 teaspoon cinnamon

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees Fahrenheit.
  2. Add oats into the food processor/blender and pulse until flour-like consistency.
  3. Place the rest of the ingredients into the blender and blend until smooth.
  4. Spoon batter evenly into a muffin pan, about 1/2 full.
  5. Bake for 20-25 minutes, until inserted toothpick comes out clean.
  6. Cool in the pan for about 10 minutes. Transfer to a rack to cool completely. See storage tips in the notes section below
