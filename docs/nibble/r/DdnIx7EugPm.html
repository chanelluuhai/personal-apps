Toddler Korean Shrimp Pancake or Jeon (새우전)

From @busymamaeasybites on Instagram · 2026-09-23

Ingredients

  • 9 shrimp — I used @costco Argentine wild-caught shrimp
  • Finely chopped onion
  • Finely chopped zucchini
  • Finely chopped carrot
  • 1 egg
  • Garlic powder
  • 1–2 tbsp cornstarch or potato starch, as needed
  • Olive oil for cooking

Steps

  1. Finely mince the raw shrimp into very small pieces.
  2. Finely chop the onion, zucchini, and carrot.
  3. Add the shrimp and veggies to a bowl with the egg, a sprinkle of garlic powder, and enough cornstarch or potato starch to help everything hold together.
  4. Mix until well combined.
  5. Heat a little olive oil in a pan over low-medium heat.
  6. Spoon small portions into the pan and gently flatten into toddler-sized pancakes.
  7. Cook on both sides until golden brown and the shrimp is fully cooked through.
  8. Let cool and serve! We added ketchup for dipping because apparently everything tastes better when you can dip it.
