These are my go-to chicken meatballs when I want protein and veggies in one bite

From @soniamazri on TikTok · 2025-12-22 · 15 min

Ingredients

  • 1 cup broccoli
  • ½ cup cheddar cheese
  • 1 lb chicken breast
  • ½ cup breadcrumbs
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • ¼ tsp turmeric
  • ¼ tsp oregano .Add salt if baby is 1+

Steps

  1. Blend everything in a food processor, form into meatballs, and bake.
  2. Bake at 375°F (190°C) for 15 minutes until cooked through.
