Parmesan Chicken Meatballs

From @soniamazri on TikTok · 2026-08-25

Ingredients

  • Meatballs
  • 1 lb ground chicken breast
  • ½ cup grated Parmesan
  • ¼ cup breadcrumbs
  • 1 egg
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 tsp oregano or Italian seasoning
  • Salt (1 year+ only)
  • Black pepper Sauce
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • ½ cup grated Parmesan
  • 4 cups chopped spinach

Steps

  1. Mix and shape the meatballs
  2. Cook until golden
  3. Make the creamy spinach Parmesan sauce
  4. Return the meatballs to the pan and simmer until coated
