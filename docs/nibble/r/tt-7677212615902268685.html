Cheesy Beef Tortilla Triangles

From @soniamazri on TikTok · 2026-08-29 · 15 min

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 4 tortillas (cut in half)
  • 3 tbsp marinara sauce
  • 1½ cups mozzarella cheese
  • ½ cup cheddar cheese
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • ½ tsp garlic powder
  • ½ tsp onion powder
  • ½ tsp paprika
  • ½ tsp oregano
  • Salt (1 year+ only)
  • Black pepper

Steps

  1. Cook the ground beef with the seasonings.
  2. Fill each tortilla half with marinara sauce, mozzarella, and beef, then fold into triangles.
  3. Mix melted butter with garlic powder and oregano, spread some in the baking dish, arrange the triangles, brush the tops with the remaining butter, sprinkle with mozzarella and cheddar, and bake at 400°F (200°C) for 10–15 minutes until golden and crispy
